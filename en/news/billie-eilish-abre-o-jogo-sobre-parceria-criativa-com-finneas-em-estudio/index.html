Billie Eilish opens up about creative partnership with Finneas in the studio

At a recent event in London, the singer discussed the challenges of songwriting and the strong dynamic with her brother and producer.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Jul 11, 2025 · 2 min read

During her visit to London in early July 2025, Billie Eilish took part in an exclusive Q&A session with presenter Edith Bowman. The intimate gathering brought together one hundred fans at a venue on Old Street for a conversation focused on the creative process of the Grammy-winning artist.

At the event, the voice behind Hit Me Hard and Soft delved into details about her long-standing professional relationship with her older brother and producer, Finneas O'Connell. The duo co-writes the star's entire catalog, from the initial success of Ocean Eyes in 2015 to the most recent releases from her third studio album, dropped in May 2024.

According to the singer, family life has shaped her capacity for artistic collaboration over the years. She emphasized that mutual frankness and the lack of filters in their sibling relationship allow for a level of sincerity unattainable in other musical partnerships, guaranteeing total freedom in the studio.

Despite their enviable synergy, Billie confessed that the act of songwriting remains one of the greatest challenges of her artistic routine. She admitted that the creative process frequently causes her frustration and self-doubt, requiring persistence to translate feelings into precise lyrics and melodies, even after years of industry experience.

The conversation in London took place amidst the British and Irish leg of the world tour promoting her latest album, the successor to acclaimed previous projects. The artist's concert schedule has featured memorable moments, including surprises in the setlist that delight international audiences.

Recently, the tour also drew attention on social media following a special performance in Paris, where the group executed an acoustic version of a Paramore classic. The tribute resonated widely and received public praise from the lead singer of the original band herself, further consolidating the mutual admiration between the artists.
