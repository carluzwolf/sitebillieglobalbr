Billie Eilish and BAPE launch collaborative clothing and accessories collection

The artist has joined forces with the iconic Japanese brand for an exclusive line of apparel and accessories.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Nov 13, 2026 · 1 min read

Billie Eilish's long-standing passion for the Japanese streetwear brand A Bathing Ape (BAPE) has finally resulted in an official partnership. On November 13, a limited collection of clothing and accessories developed jointly by the singer and the fashion label was launched, uniting the artist's unmistakable aesthetic with the brand's urban DNA.

World-renowned for her authentic and oversized style, Billie has frequently incorporated BAPE pieces into her looks over the years. This long-standing relationship with the brand now gains a special chapter, materializing a moment when her personal taste transforms into an official fashion collaboration.

The limited line features a variety of apparel and accessories that blend BAPE's classic visual elements with Billie Eilish's striking identity. Among the highlights of the collection, two exclusive pieces were made available for purchase directly through the Complex platform.

The reception among fans and streetwear enthusiasts was immediate, highlighting how Billie's influence in the fashion world continues to expand. The artist, who has already signed partnerships with several major global brands, further consolidates her space as a true style icon of this generation.

To date, there is no official information detailing whether the collection will be restocked or distributed through channels other than the initial launch platform. For fans wanting to acquire the items, it remains necessary to follow the brand's official channels to check for any updates regarding international shipping.

Meanwhile, the singer continues to reap the rewards of her most recent musical phase and keep fans attentive to her every new endeavor, whether in music or the fashion universe. We will continue to closely follow all of Billie Eilish's steps and future partnerships right here at Billie Global BR.
