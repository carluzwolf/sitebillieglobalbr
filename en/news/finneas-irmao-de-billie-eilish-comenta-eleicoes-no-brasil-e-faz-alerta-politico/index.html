Finneas, Billie Eilish's brother, comments on elections in Brazil and issues political warning on social media

The producer and musician used Instagram to discuss the Brazilian electoral scenario and relate the situation to United States politics.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Oct 7, 2026 · 2 min read

Producer, singer, and songwriter Finneas, widely known for his creative partnership and family bond with Billie Eilish, once again caught the attention of the Brazilian public by speaking out about the country's politics. Through his social media, the artist shared reflections regarding the outcome of the first stage of the national election and drew direct parallels with recent events on North American soil.

Using the stories feature on his official Instagram profile, the musician addressed the possibility of Flávio Bolsonaro reaching the head of the Brazilian federal Executive branch. In the post, he related the local scenario to Donald Trump's return to the presidency of the United States, suggesting that the repetition of conservative administrations brings even more severe consequences in subsequent terms.

In his message published online, Billie Eilish's brother pointed out that the North American experience serves as a warning to the Brazilian electorate. The producer emphasized that the second opportunity granted to this profile of leadership tends to be even worse for society, reflecting his constant critical stance on international political agendas.

This is not the first time the artist has publicly engaged with Brazil's political landscape. During the 2022 presidential race, which pitted Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva and Jair Bolsonaro against each other in the runoff, the singer used his digital platforms to declare his vote and explicit support for the current Brazilian president.

The recent statement comes a few days after the musician announced the cancellation of his participation as an opening act for Ed Sheeran's shows, which will take place in São Paulo in December. Initially scheduled to warm up the audience before the performances at Nubank Parque, he chose to withdraw from the concert schedule.

At the time he made his withdrawal from the tour public, Finneas argued that music professionals must have total freedom to defend oppressed populations, reiterating on that occasion his stance in favor of Palestine. The departure of Billie Eilish's brother from the concert itinerary happened shortly after rapper Macklemore also decided to leave the British singer's same tour.

Meanwhile, Brazilian fans of Billie Eilish and Finneas continue to closely follow the duo's steps, whether in the strictly musical sphere or in the public discussions raised by the artists. With a gigantic and engaged fan base in the country, each stance resonates intensely on the local community's social networks.
