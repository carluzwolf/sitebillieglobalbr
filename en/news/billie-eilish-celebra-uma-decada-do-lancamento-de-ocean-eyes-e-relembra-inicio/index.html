Billie Eilish celebrates a decade since the release of Ocean Eyes and looks back at the beginning of her career with Finneas

The artist and her brother marked ten years of the hit that kicked off the pop star's global journey.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Nov 19, 2025 · 2 min read

The journey of one of contemporary pop music's greatest voices has reached an impressive milestone. Billie Eilish officially celebrated a decade since her life and her brother's changed completely with the arrival of a single track on digital platforms.

View post on Instagram ↗

It all started casually when Ocean Eyes was made available on the internet in November 2015. Composed and produced by Finneas initially for his band, the song ended up being recorded by his sister, who at the time was only 13 years old, so she could practice a dance choreography.

What was just a file shared on SoundCloud quickly gained global proportions, turning into the starting point of a cultural phenomenon. To celebrate the round anniversary, Finneas took to social media to rescue memories of Billie's adolescence and reflect on their journey together in the music industry.

In his Instagram post, the producer highlighted how the track shaped both of their youth and accompanied them through ten years of learning in songwriting, production, and adult life itself. He also described the creative partnership as a dream job that allowed them to travel the world together.

Billie made sure to repost her brother's tribute on her stories, reinforcing the impact that the music had on her career. The singer summarized the moment by declaring that it all started because of that song, acknowledging the historic weight the track holds in her artistic history.

Throughout this decade, the young star has accumulated extraordinary achievements that redefined the standards of the recording industry. Among her most expressive awards are nine Grammy statuettes, including the historic feat of dominating the four main categories of the awards ceremony in 2020 all at once.

In addition to her resounding success in music, the star has also accumulated two Oscar wins in the Best Original Song category for tracks with a major cinematic impact. With impressive Guinness records and a passionate global fan base, Billie Eilish continues to consolidate her legacy as one of the most creative and influential minds of her generation.
