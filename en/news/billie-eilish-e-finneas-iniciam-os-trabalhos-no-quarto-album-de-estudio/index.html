Billie Eilish and Finneas begin work on fourth studio album

In a recent interview, Billie Eilish confirmed that she is already in the studio with her brother Finneas working on her next musical project.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Oct 23, 2026 · 1 min read

The creativity of Billie Eilish and Finneas O'Connell seems to have no end. In a recent interview granted to Vogue magazine, the Oscar-winning artist revealed that she and her brother and long-time producer have already started preparations for the development of their fourth studio album.

The news excites fans around the world, coming after the huge success of the album Hit Me Hard and Soft, released in May 2024. That project introduced the public to ten striking tracks, including global hits like Birds of a Feather, Wildflower, Lunch, and Chihiro, further consolidating the musical partnership between the siblings.

Maintaining the creative dynamic that has accompanied the duo since the beginning of their career, Finneas once again takes on production and shares the songwriting with the singer. When asked about the progress of the creation process, Billie highlighted the duo's total dedication to the new material at the present moment.

Despite the confirmation that they are actively producing, the project still remains surrounded by great mystery. To date, the artist's team has not released an official title, tracklist, cover art, or release date for the successor to Hit Me Hard and Soft.

Rumors from the international press, specifically from the newspaper The Sun, recently speculated about a supposed advanced stage of recordings and possible tour plans for the coming years. However, it is important to emphasize that none of this information has been officially confirmed by the singer's press office or her record label.

While admirers await concrete news and official announcements coming directly from Billie Eilish, it remains to follow the developments of this new artistic phase. Billie Global BR will actively keep an eye on all updates regarding the future of our idol.
