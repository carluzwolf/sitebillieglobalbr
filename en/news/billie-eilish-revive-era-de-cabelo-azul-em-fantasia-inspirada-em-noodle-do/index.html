Billie Eilish revives blue hair era in Noodle-inspired Gorillaz costume for Halloween

The singer surprised fans by adopting electric blue locks in a creative outfit for Halloween, weeks after sporting a strawberry blonde bob cut.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Nov 5, 2026 · 2 min read

Billie Eilish's creativity for Halloween always makes headlines in the international press and on social media, and this year's celebration was no exception. Known for her authenticity when putting together themed looks, the artist chose to pay tribute to an iconic figure of pop culture by recreating the look of the character Noodle, from the famous virtual band Gorillaz.

To compose the costume to perfection, the Hit Me Hard and Soft singer surprised the public by bringing back one of her past trademarks: electric blue hair. The aesthetic choice immediately transported nostalgic fans back to the star's early career, a time when vibrant tones in her locks were her unmistakable visual signature.

The transformation drew even more attention because of the recent timing of the hair change. Shortly before the Halloween festivities, Billie had been seen sporting a chin-length strawberry blonde bob, a praised style that marked yet another phase of transition in her look over recent months.

Although the artist does not usually detail every step of her festive productions publicly, photos and videos shared by friends and guests revealed the details of the characterization. The attention to the characteristic visual elements of the Gorillaz guitarist demonstrated the singer's care for musical references that influence her.

The festive disguise reinforces Eilish's versatility with her own image, proving that she easily transitions between modern trends and nostalgic references. For the fans who follow every change in the artist's routine, the news was celebrated as a fun gift amid the busy schedule of the world tour.

So far, representatives for the artist have not disclosed whether the electric blue hair is here to stay permanently or if it was just a temporary solution for the festive date. While new developments regarding the look are not officially confirmed, fans continue to buzz about the tribute and remember the most striking moments of the blue aesthetic in the Grammy winner's trajectory.
