Fact Check: Fake news goes viral on social media about Billie Eilish

A fake post recently circulated on social media falsely claiming that singer Billie Eilish had made an announcement regarding a gender transition.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Nov 7, 2026 · 1 min read

In recent days, a wave of false rumors took over platforms like X, Facebook, and Instagram, involving the name of singer Billie Eilish. Several profiles began sharing manipulated screenshots that simulated a supposed official statement published on the artist's stories, generating strong repercussions and confusion among fans.

The fabricated material brought untruthful claims that the star was undergoing a gender transition, in addition to announcing the end of her relationship with actor Nat Wolff. The fake post quickly accumulated millions of views and divided opinions on the internet, with many followers expressing concern and demanding an immediate verification of the facts.

Fact-checking professionals and press outlets confirmed that the story is entirely invented. A thorough check of the singer's official profiles and news portal records proves that such a statement was never made by her on any social media.

The rumor also fails to hold up against the couple's recent history, given that there is no real indication of a breakup between Eilish and Wolff. The two publicly acknowledged their romance in the first half of 2026 and remain together, disproving any separation narrative circulating on the web.

Fans and admirers of the artist are always advised to seek information from official and reliable sources before sharing screenshots or sensationalist rumors. The proliferation of fake news on the internet continues to be a recurring challenge, requiring caution from the public in the face of unverified publications.
