Billie Eilish surprises fans with rare rock star look in Halloween costume

Known for her authenticity in fashion and for setting trends since the beginning of her musical trajectory with her debut EP and the acclaimed album When We All Fall Asleep, Where Do We Go?, the singer decided to pay homage to one of her great childhood musical influences.
