Billie Eilish is honored with a grove named after her in Austin

The artist received a green space dedicated to her at Zilker Park in Austin, recognizing her strong environmental activism.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Oct 29, 2026 · 1 min read

Billie Eilish's environmental activism has received a definitive and lasting tribute in the state of Texas. The American singer had a grove named in her honor inside the traditional Zilker Park, located in the city of Austin. The initiative celebrates the artist's constant engagement in favor of ecological causes and sustainability in the entertainment industry.

Known worldwide not only for her musical hits, but also for her firm stance regarding the climate crisis, Billie has used her massive platform to encourage sustainable practices. She frequently promotes ecological initiatives on her global tours, such as reducing single-use plastic, partnerships with environmental organizations, and encouraging plant-based food options for the audience and crews.

Zilker Park is one of the most loved and visited public spaces in the Texas capital, functioning as the green heart of the city and hosting large-scale events throughout the year. The choice of the location for the naming of the grove reflects the positive impact that the artist's voice has generated on new generations, inspiring fans to adopt more conscious habits with the planet.

This tribute adds to a trajectory marked by awards and international recognition, which now also encompasses the socio-environmental field. Although specific details about the official inauguration and the botanical characteristics of the space have not yet been fully disclosed by local authorities, the news is already having a positive repercussion among the singer's admirers.

For fans of Billie Global BR, the news reinforces the pride of following an artist who transcends music and uses her visibility to transform the world around her. Billie Eilish's dedication to environmental preservation continues to prove that art and ecological responsibility can walk together, leaving a lasting legacy for the next generations.
