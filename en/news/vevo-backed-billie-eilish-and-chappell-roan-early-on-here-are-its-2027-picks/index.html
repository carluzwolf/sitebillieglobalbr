Vevo looks back at Billie Eilish's time on musical breakthrough program

The platform revealed its new list of promises for 2027 and highlighted the success history of former participants, including the owner of Hit Me Hard and Soft.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Nov 10, 2026 · 2 min read

Music video platform Vevo officially released its new selection of breakthrough acts for the DSCVR Artists to Watch program, aimed at emerging talents who promise to dominate the industry in the coming months. Reaching its thirteenth edition, the initiative has built a solid reputation over the years for identifying promising names before they achieve global stardom in pop music and other genres.

Among the major highlights in the platform's history, Vevo frequently looks back at artists who today draw crowds and dominate charts around the world. Heavyweights like Sam Smith, Lewis Capaldi, Ice Spice, and Chappell Roan have passed through previous editions of the project at decisive moments in their respective trajectories in the recording industry.

Billie Eilish joined the program in 2018, when she was still taking firm steps toward the international recognition she would soon achieve with her first high-impact releases. The artist's journey often serves as an example of the rigor and precision of the curation carried out by the video company's specialists.

The selection process for the 2027 list involved analyzing over six hundred names submitted from various parts of the world. Curators, programmers, and record label representatives debated streaming metrics, social media growth, and audience engagement to define the eighteen acts selected for this new class.

The chosen artists participate in exclusive recording sessions organized by the company in designated studios, producing visual performances aimed at digital promotion. In addition, the material is featured on connected TV networks and in strategic ads broadcast on the YouTube platform.

This means fans watching music videos by established artists, such as Billie Eilish herself, may come across previews and recommendations of the new talents before the main content. The strategy aims to expose newcomers to millions of daily views, significantly boosting their careers in the current music scene.

The most recent list covers a significant variety of styles, ranging from modern country and alternative rock to trap and Latin bachata. With this geographical and sonic diversity, Vevo reinforces its ongoing commitment to anticipating trends and introducing the next great voices in music to the global audience.
