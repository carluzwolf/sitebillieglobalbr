Billie Eilish debuts new red hair and shares kisses with Nat Wolff in Los Angeles

In a rare public appearance, the singer and the actor were photographed buying Halloween candy and enjoying romantic moments.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Nov 2, 2026 · 2 min read

Billie Eilish took advantage of some downtime to enjoy a relaxed stroll through the streets of Studio City, in Los Angeles, alongside her boyfriend, actor and musician Nat Wolff. Known for keeping her love life out of the spotlight, the pop star caught the attention of passersby not only for her company, but also for showing off her new short red hair, a shade she had recently debuted on her social media.

During the walk through the Californian neighborhood, the couple was seen visiting a commercial establishment to secure treats and festive Halloween-themed items. The artist opted for an entirely casual look and skipped makeup, maintaining discretion while choosing products alongside her partner, with whom she maintains a relationship marked by privacy.

After finishing their shopping, the two walked to the singer's vehicle, an electric Porsche Taycan, where they placed the bags in the trunk. Before wrapping up the outing and driving away, Billie and Nat shared affectionate moments and passionate kisses right in the parking lot, showing great chemistry in a rare moment of relaxation captured by photographers' lenses.

The relationship journey between the voice behind *Hit Me Hard and Soft* and the artist began in 2023, when their paths crossed during the Academy Museum Gala in California. The connection between them strengthened with the support of Alex Wolff, Nat's brother, who already knew the singer and publicly pointed out that they all share similar experiences dealing with a Tourette's syndrome diagnosis.

The bond between them grew even closer the following year, when they attended the Coachella Festival together and Nat joined the cast of the *Chihiro* music video. The partnership also extended to the road during the singer's world tour that began in September 2024, a period in which the Wolff brothers accompanied several shows and the actor heaped praise on his girlfriend's artistic genius in interviews.

Despite fan speculation that had circulated for months, the romance entered the international spotlight after the couple was spotted in a romantic mood in Italy. Since then, the duo has taken important steps in their public relationship, including a striking red carpet debut during the premiere of the concert film for the singer's most recent tour, consolidating their union before the public and the media.
