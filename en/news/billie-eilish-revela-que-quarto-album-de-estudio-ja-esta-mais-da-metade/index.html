Billie Eilish reveals that fourth studio album is already more than half completed

In a recent interview, the singer confirmed that her next record is on the way and teased news about plans for the future.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Apr 28, 2026 · 2 min read

Excitement for Billie Eilish's new musical era continues to grow among fans around the world. In an exclusive interview released this Tuesday (28), the North American artist opened up about the behind-the-scenes of her career and brought excellent news for the fandom, confirming that the successor to her recent works is already taking shape in a very advanced way.

Speaking with ELLE magazine, the pop star discussed the progress of her fourth studio album and assured that the project is very close to becoming a reality. When asked if the unreleased material is on the way, she answered positively and revealed that the record has already passed the mark of being half-way completed production-wise.

So far, further details about the sound concept, the official title, or an exact release date have not yet been released by the singer's official teams. The successor will arrive to follow up on the acclaimed album Hit Me Hard and Soft, released in 2024 and responsible for landing global hits like Lunch and Birds of a Feather on music charts across the planet.

In addition to commenting on the creative process in music, the chat also featured lighthearted moments about the artist's personal life. In a humorous tone, Billie talked about astrology and shared past romantic experiences, amusing readers with her sincere opinions about zodiac signs.

The singer also took the opportunity to ponder her next steps in the universe of cinema and acting. Although she showed great interest in acting in future audiovisual projects, she weighed that taking on roles such as directing and screenwriting requires an immense level of effort, preferring to focus initially on acting.

While fans await more news about the fourth studio album, the artist's schedule remains packed with major moments for the coming months. Among the highlights is the arrival of the concert film Billie Eilish – Hit Me Hard and Soft: The Tour in 3D, co-directed by James Cameron, with its theatrical premiere in Brazil scheduled for the month of May.
