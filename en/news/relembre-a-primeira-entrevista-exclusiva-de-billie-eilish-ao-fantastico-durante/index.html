Remembering Billie Eilish's first exclusive interview with Fantástico during her visit to Lollapalooza Brazil

In 2023, the artist spoke with the Sunday show in São Paulo, answered questions sent by admirers, and celebrated the warmth of the national public.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Mar 26, 2023 · 2 min read

Billie Eilish's visit to Brazil in March 2023 left a deep mark on the journey of the artist and her Brazilian admirers. As the main headliner of that year's edition of the Lollapalooza festival, held in São Paulo, the singer took advantage of her stay in the capital of São Paulo to grant an exclusive interview to the program Fantástico, broadcast by Rede Globo.

The conversation was led by journalist Álvaro Pereira Júnior at a hotel in the city, right after the North American star's first major show in the country. For the occasion, the show's production gathered dozens of questions sent by followers from different states via social media, creating a direct connection between the voice of "bad guy" and her passionate fanbase on Brazilian soil.

During the relaxed chat, the multi-Grammy-winning young artist opened her heart about the overwhelming energy she encountered upon landing in national territory. The star pointed out that the first social media page dedicated to her was created in the country, which reinforced her emotional bond with Brazilians. At the time, at 21 years old, she had already accumulated historic achievements, including an Oscar in the best original song category.

The conversation also touched upon landmark moments of her discography, highlighting tracks that defined important phases of her personal and artistic evolution. The Californian songwriter reflected on how the creative process, carried out in partnership with her brother and producer Finneas, reflected her real experiences and feelings while growing up under the spotlight of global fame.

Another emotional point of the interview happened when the artist imagined a dialogue with her younger self. She confessed that all the goals set in childhood and adolescence came true far beyond any expectation she could have imagined at the beginning of her journey in the music industry.

The interview, although subject to the common restrictions of major international tours regarding future studio projects, served to bring the musician even closer to her audience in Latin America. The historic visit solidified her status as an icon of the current generation and left unforgettable memories for those who followed the special coverage on open television and digital platforms.
