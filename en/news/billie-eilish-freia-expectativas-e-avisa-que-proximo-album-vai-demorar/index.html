Billie Eilish tempers expectations and warns that next album will take time

In a recent interview, the singer revealed that she is halfway through the production of the new record, but asked fans for patience.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · May 7, 2026 · 2 min read

The era of the acclaimed album "HIT ME HARD AND SOFT" continues to mark the pop star's recent journey, but admirers are already beginning to speculate about the artist's next steps. Known for her authenticity and deep connection with the public, the singer has shared some details about the progress of her future creative projects.

During an appearance on host Kent Small's podcast, the voice behind "BIRDS OF A FEATHER" opened up about the current stage of her new compositions. Directly questioned about the creation process, she confirmed that the unreleased material has already advanced quite a bit.

According to the artist herself, the current project is halfway done, with a significant portion of the tracks already structured. However, when challenged to summarize the sound or essence of what is to come in just one word, she admitted that it is still too early to define the exact direction of the work.

Despite feeding followers' curiosity by revealing that the work is progressing well, the singer showed a certain discomfort with the pressure exerted by the public and the media. She pointed out that any mention of the songs' progress usually generates an immediate wave of theories and exaggerated expectations about imminent releases.

To dispel any rumors that a release might be close, the star was categorical in stating that the album will not reach the public in the very near future. With good humor and sincerity, she asked fans to give her space to develop the repertoire calmly.

Faced with the definitive response, the host suggested that the Grammy winner take a prolonged break to relax and enjoy some rest before diving fully into the final stretch of recordings. Although she acknowledged that there is still a lot of hard work ahead, the musician reaffirmed her enthusiasm for what she has been creating in the studio.

While the new material does not get an official release date, fans continue to follow every step of the artist, celebrating the legacy of her previous releases and waiting patiently for the future musical news to come.
