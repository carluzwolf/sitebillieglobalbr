Billie Eilish and Finneas launch sustainable jewelry collection in partnership with John Hardy

The famous sibling duo expands their creativity into the fashion world by co-signing an exclusive line of eco-friendly accessories.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Oct 21, 2026 · 2 min read

Known worldwide for dominating the music charts and winning prestigious awards, Billie Eilish and Finneas have decided to explore a new creative territory. While preparing new studio projects for fans, the siblings surprised the public by announcing an unprecedented collaboration in the universe of fine jewelry. The venture consists of a complete line of accessories developed in partnership with the renowned brand John Hardy.

The project was divided into two distinct lines that reflect the duo's complementary personalities. The first line, named Wildflower as a direct reference to one of the artist's musical hits, focuses on delicate floral motifs and recycled sterling silver. Meanwhile, the second collection, called Screwball, explores sculptural chains and geometric shapes, bringing a bolder and more urban aesthetic for those who like to mix pieces.

The creation process happened very organically, with the singer testing various items directly on stage during her recent tours. According to the siblings, the priority was to ensure that the accessories were comfortable for daily wear and did not cause discomfort during energetic performances. This practical testing phase helped shape the weight and final shape of each ring, necklace, and bracelet.

Another fundamental pillar for the duo was the undeniable commitment to sustainability and environmental preservation. The entire collection was crafted using exclusively reclaimed materials and lab-created diamonds, which have a lower ecological impact. For the musicians, who frequently support environmental causes on their tours, it was essential that the manufacturing process was aligned with their personal values.

The initiative also marks a striking evolution in the artists' personal style over the years. Known at the beginning of her career for super oversized looks and lots of flashy jewelry, the singer now prefers more discreet accessories for everyday life. Finneas, in turn, has adopted a minimalist look and saw in the pieces an elegant way to complement more basic outfits.

The complete line is now available on the international market with prices that vary considerably depending on the chosen piece. Although the current focus is the partnership with the jewelry brand, the siblings do not rule out the possibility of getting involved in future design projects. For now, fans of the duo can celebrate this fashion novelty while anxiously awaiting news in their music careers.
