Billie Eilish surprises fans by showing off new short hair and strawberry-blonde tone

The singer opted for a bold hair transformation and sparked fun debates on social media about her resemblance to her brother, Finneas.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Oct 22, 2026 · 2 min read

Singer Billie Eilish caused a stir among her millions of followers by posting a new video on her official Instagram account. In the recent post, the 24-year-old artist fully revealed her new look, showing off short locks in a light red shade known as strawberry-blonde. The radical change quickly caught the public's attention, stirring up social media with thousands of reactions and comments about the pop star's new aesthetic.

World-renowned for her constant and striking hair transformations throughout her career, Billie has already tried several iconic colors, such as the neon green that marked the era of her debut album, the platinum blonde of the Happier Than Ever phase, as well as stints with black and dark brown. This time, the choice for a much shorter cut and a shade leaning toward light red renewed the singer's image, marking yet another chapter in her aesthetic journey.

As soon as the video was posted, the internet wasted no time and the comment section was taken over by lighthearted reactions. Many fans and admirers began to joke that, with the new haircut and revamped look, Billie was increasingly resembling her brother and longtime musical partner, Finneas O'Connell. The good-humored comparisons between the siblings quickly became one of the most talked-about topics on social media, amusing the global fandom.

Despite the fans' affectionate jokes about the family resemblance, the reception to the new look was overwhelmingly positive. Admirers from all over the world praised the singer's authenticity and highlighted how she manages to visually reinvent herself with every new phase of her career. Billie Eilish's versatility in setting trends and surprising the public with bold choices continues to be one of her trademarks.

Currently, Billie Eilish continues to reap the rewards of her solid musical journey, marked by streaming records, major awards, and highly successful tours around the globe. While fans continue to follow the artist's every step on social media, it remains to be seen whether the new strawberry-blonde look is here to stay or if it is part of the preparation for the singer's future artistic projects.
