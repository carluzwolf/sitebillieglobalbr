Billie Eilish and Quenlin Blackwell reveal character crushes in fun interview

The singer participated in Complex's famous GOAT Talk segment alongside her friend Quenlin Blackwell to debate the best and worst possible topics.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Nov 6, 2026 · 2 min read

Singer Billie Eilish recently participated in another edition of the popular GOAT Talk segment, produced by the entertainment magazine and portal Complex. On the occasion, the interpreter of What Was I Made For? was accompanied by her close friend, influencer and content creator Quenlin Blackwell, with whom she frequently shares fun moments on social media.

During the recording, the duo was challenged to debate and choose the best and worst items from various unusual categories, following the show's relaxed format. Amidst laughter and many jokes, the two talked about personal preferences, pop culture, and everyday experiences, showing the bond they share off stage and out of the spotlight.

One of the highlights of the conversation happened when Billie and Quenlin revealed who their biggest fictional crushes would be. The moment drew laughter from the crew and generated a huge reaction among fans on digital platforms, who quickly began commenting on the unusual choices made by the two friends during the dynamic.

Known for her authentic style and for maintaining a very close relationship with her followers, the artist used the space to show a lighter and more playful side. Participations in fast-paced entertainment formats like the one by Complex allow the public to get to know the pop star's personality better away from the pressure of major music releases and world tours.

The joint appearance also reinforces the strong friendship between Billie and Quenlin, who frequently appear together at events and online publications. For fans of Billie Eilish, seeing the singer so relaxed is always a treat, especially at a time of consolidation of her career after the enormous success of her studio albums and historic achievements in the industry.

The full interview is part of a series of promotional actions and special content released by Complex, which also took the opportunity to highlight official products and exclusive collections associated with the singer's image in its online store. While admirers await new musical projects, content like this guarantees quality entertainment and brings the artist even closer to her global fan base.
