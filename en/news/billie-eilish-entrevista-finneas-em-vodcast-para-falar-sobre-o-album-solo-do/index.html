Billie Eilish interviews Finneas on vodcast to talk about her brother's solo album

In a special Spotify episode, Billie Eilish hosted a relaxed conversation with Finneas about the release of his second solo record, For Cryin' Out Loud!.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Oct 2, 2024 · 3 min read

The musical relationship between Billie Eilish and Finneas is one of the most successful and awarded partnerships in the contemporary music industry. In addition to creating global hits together and winning multiple awards, such as Grammys and Oscars, the siblings continue to deeply support each other's individual projects. An example of this happened recently when Billie took on the role of interviewer in a special episode of the Countdown To vodcast, broadcast by Spotify.

The main focus of the conversation was Finneas's second solo album, titled For Cryin' Out Loud!, which reached the public shortly after the recording. During the chat, the singer wanted to know what her brother's expectations were and what he hoped the audience would feel when listening to the new tracks. With good humor, the producer joked about his follower base and expressed surprise upon revealing that tickets for his intimate shows in cities like Los Angeles, New York, and London sold out quickly.

Unlike his debut record, Optimist, released in 2021 in a quite solitary way, the new project bet on collective energy. Finneas explained that the album was born from jam sessions with close friends. Billie reinforced the idea by commenting on the fun of playing in a group, highlighting that this approach brought a completely new atmosphere to the creative process of the musician, who has produced his sister's albums for years.

The conversation also made room to recall memorable moments from the longtime partnership between the two. Finneas highlighted the creation of the track What Was I Made For? as one of his favorite processes in his career, besides commenting on the remix of Guess, a collaboration with Charli XCX that featured Billie's participation. The sibling dynamic was evident when the artist admitted that, although it is hard to see her brother step away from rehearsals, she fully supports his solo journey so that he can have fun.

While Finneas promotes his new work, Billie Eilish continues with her busy schedule. The singer recently kicked off her eagerly awaited world tour Hit Me Hard and Soft: The Tour, passing through sold-out arenas in North America to promote her acclaimed third studio album, released this year. The tour further solidifies the moment of great prominence that both are experiencing in their respective paths in music.

Alongside stage commitments, new horizons are opening up for the artist. In recent interviews given to specialized outlets, Finneas revealed that he has been setting up a home studio for Billie with the goal of teaching her to produce her own music independently. The producer praised his sister's natural talent and explained that he prefers to introduce basic tools so she can develop her skills at her own pace.

This individual artistic evolution further strengthens the partnership that started in a teenager's bedroom and turned into a global phenomenon. With successful albums, international tours, and consolidated side projects, Billie Eilish and Finneas continue to redefine the directions of their careers, maintaining the complicity that has captivated fans worldwide.
