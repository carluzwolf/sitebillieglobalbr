Billie Eilish Celebrates Finneas and Claudia Sulewski's Love as Officiant in Emotional Wedding

The singer officiated her brother's union in California, in a ceremony that brought together major music and film celebrities.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Sep 1, 2026 · 2 min read

The partnership and complicity between Billie Eilish and her brother and producer, Finneas O'Connell, has gone beyond the stages and recording studios to reach an unforgettable chapter in the family's personal life. The artist took on a very special role by officiating the marriage of Finneas to his long-time partner, influencer and actress Claudia Sulewski.

The exclusive event took place at the prestigious San Ysidro Ranch, located in Santa Barbara, California. The paradisiacal and private setting hosted an intimate celebration that brought together family members and a select group of famous friends to witness the couple's exchange of rings.

For the festive occasion, the Hit Me Hard and Soft singer chose a striking look, consisting of a moss-green dress with a neckline and structured corset, complemented by her red hair. During the reading of the vows, emotion took over the artist, who couldn't hold back her tears in front of her brother's affectionate words.

In addition to the strong presence of the O'Connell family, the guest list featured several heavyweights from the entertainment industry. Celebrities such as Jason Sudeikis, Kristen Bell, and Nina Dobrev were present, along with the couple Justin and Hailey Bieber, who also caught the attention of the audience.

During the event, images of Justin and Hailey generated great repercussion on social media after they were caught in a moment of apparent discussion among the guests. In the footage shared online, both appear gesturing seriously before stepping away briefly.

Despite the momentary friction recorded by photographers and guests, the celebratory atmosphere prevailed for the rest of the event. The singer later shared pictures alongside his wife, showing that harmony was restored in the union they formalized in 2018, which now also features the company of their son Jack Blues Bieber.

For Billie Eilish fans, seeing the singer take charge of such a landmark moment in the life of her main musical collaborator reinforces the deep bonds that unite the siblings. Finneas and Claudia's wedding went down in family history and into the repertoire of cherished memories for the duo's admirers.
