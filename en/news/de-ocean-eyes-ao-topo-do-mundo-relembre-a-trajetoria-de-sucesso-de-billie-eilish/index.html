From Ocean Eyes to the top of the world: look back at Billie Eilish's successful trajectory

The Billie Global BR portal prepared a complete overview of Billie Eilish's artistic evolution, from her first steps in music to her consolidation as one of the generation's greatest icons.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Nov 5, 2026 · 2 min read

Billie Eilish Pirate Baird O'Connell's journey to global stardom is one of contemporary music's most fascinating stories. Raised in Los Angeles alongside her brother and creative partner Finneas O'Connell, the artist had an arts-focused upbringing, which encouraged her artistic autonomy since childhood and paved the way for a unique and innovative sound.

The turning point happened in 2015, when the siblings casually released the track Ocean Eyes on the SoundCloud platform. The composition, originally intended for Billie's dance class, quickly went viral and captured the attention of the public and the record industry, transforming the young singer into an alternative pop music promise overnight.

The resounding success on the internet opened doors for the acclaimed debut EP, Don't Smile at Me, released in 2017, which charted hits like Ocean Eyes and Bellyache. However, it was with her full-length debut album, When We All Fall Asleep, Where Do We Go?, in 2019, that the artist redefined global pop culture standards, mixing elements of electropop, trap, and melancholic lyrics.

Absolute consecration came at the 2020 Grammy ceremony, when Billie made history by winning the award's four main categories on the same night. This symbolic achievement crowned an artist who rejected the traditional molds of fame, betting on a unique visual style, with baggy clothes and a striking personality.

In the following years, the star continued to expand her artistic and thematic horizons. Her second studio album, Happier Than Ever, released in 2021, brought a more mature and reflective sound, addressing the burdens of public exposure and the challenges of growing up in the spotlight, in addition to consolidating sold-out tours around the planet.

The most recent phase of her career is marked by the album Hit Me Hard and Soft, released in 2024, and the world tour of the same name. The project reaffirms the singer's vocal versatility and compositional maturity, who has also stood out for her environmental activism and engagement in important social causes.

For Brazilian fans, following every step of this trajectory is a reason for constant pride. The artist continues to write her name in music history with authenticity, proving that her meteoric rise was just the beginning of a lasting legacy in the global recording industry.
