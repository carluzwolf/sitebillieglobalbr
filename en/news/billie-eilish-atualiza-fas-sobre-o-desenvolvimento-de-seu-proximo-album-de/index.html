Billie Eilish updates fans on the development of her next studio album

In a new statement to admirers, the singer confirmed that she is focused on creating a brand-new repertoire alongside Finneas.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Oct 22, 2026 · 2 min read

Singer Billie Eilish has brought exciting new updates for fans who are already eagerly awaiting the next steps in her discography. During a recent public interaction, the artist commented on the progress of studio work for the successor to the acclaimed album Hit Me Hard and Soft, released in 2024.

According to the songwriter, the creative process has been intense and quite productive. She highlighted that she has been spending a lot of time in the studio alongside her brother, producer, and longtime musical partner, Finneas, resulting in a large amount of unreleased material currently in development.

Despite the exciting news about the songwriting process, the artist's team has not yet released official launch dates, a tracklist, or conceptual details about the new project. Internet speculations also point to possible performances in large arenas and stadiums in the future, but such plans lack official confirmation so far.

The duo's recent track record demonstrates a steady work pace and a strong artistic connection. Since their global explosion with the debut album When We All Fall Asleep, Where Do We Go?, released in 2019, Billie and Finneas have consolidated an intimate and innovative formula that has yielded multiple Grammy awards and worldwide critical acclaim.

The release of Hit Me Hard and Soft kicked off a new era in the star's career, accompanied by a world tour that quickly sold out tickets across multiple continents. Brazilian fans are closely following every development, hoping that the next production cycle will also bring new opportunities to see the singer live in the country.

While new official announcements are not yet revealed, the recommendation for the fandom is to stay tuned to the artist's communication channels and continue celebrating the legacy of the current era. The Billie Global BR portal will continue following and translating all official updates on the future steps of our favorite fairy.
