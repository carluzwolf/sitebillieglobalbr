Billie Eilish's stances on plastic surgery recalled following new campaign

The singer's past comments on natural aging resurfaced on social media following the launch of an advertising campaign.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Oct 23, 2026 · 2 min read

Singer Billie Eilish has once again become a trending topic on social media and international entertainment portals after starring in a new advertising campaign. This time, promotional photos of the artist for the jewelry brand John Hardy sparked intense discussions among fans and the general public, with internet users raising speculations about possible physical changes in the pop star's chest area.

In light of the buzz generated online, previous statements by the multiple Grammy Award winner regarding cosmetic procedures and the beauty industry ended up resurfacing. Known for her authenticity and for maintaining a transparent relationship with her audience, the singer of global hits has always been questioned about the topic throughout her career in the music industry.

In recent podcast appearances and interviews, such as her conversation on the show Good Hang With Amy Poehler, the artist addressed the subject directly. On that occasion, she stated that she does not intend to resort to interventions such as facial fillers or plastic surgery in the future. The songwriter emphasized that she maintains a desire to age naturally and without altering her original features over the years.

During the same interview, the star explained that she wants her future children to recognize her natural traits when looking at her face. She also criticized the aesthetic pressure exerted by society and reinforced that she prefers to accept the natural aging process of the body, distancing herself from artificial standards she considers harmful promoted by the current market.

The singer's stance on the topic is nothing new to long-time fans. In an interview granted to The Guardian newspaper in 2021, the artist commented that every individual has the right to do whatever they want with their own body to feel happy. However, she pointed out that her greatest frustration is when public figures undergo cosmetic procedures and publicly deny them, setting unrealistic and potentially harmful expectations for youth and adolescents.

Since the beginning of her career, Billie Eilish's relationship with her own image has been a frequent topic of debate in the media. In the past, the artist intentionally adopted the use of loose and oversized clothing. In previous interviews for publications like Vogue Australia and Elle magazine, she explained that this style choice served as a protective barrier to prevent the public from judging her body or forming opinions about her physical shape.

To date, there is no official confirmation or statement from the singer regarding the recent rumors speculated on the internet surrounding the advertising campaign. Meanwhile, Billie Global BR fans continue to follow the artist's next steps, supporting her freedom of expression and her firm stance regarding self-acceptance and respect for her own individuality.
