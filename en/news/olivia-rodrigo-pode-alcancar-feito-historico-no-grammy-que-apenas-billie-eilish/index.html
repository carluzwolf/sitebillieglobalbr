Olivia Rodrigo could achieve a historic feat at the Grammy that only Billie Eilish managed recently

With her third album in the running, Olivia Rodrigo can match an impeccable mark that Billie Eilish achieved at the Grammys.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Nov 14, 2026 · 2 min read

The pop music world is closely following the developments of the awards season and the focus now turns to the Grammy Awards. Singer Olivia Rodrigo could achieve a very exclusive and rare feat at the ceremony, repeating a trajectory of success that has direct parallels with our Billie Eilish. The discussion revolves around the impressive consistency that both artists managed to maintain right at the beginning of their careers in the recording industry.

To understand the magnitude of the moment, we need to look at the Best Pop Vocal Album category. While most major pop stars end up missing out on this specific race with at least one of their early projects, a few exceptions manage to maintain a totally flawless streak since their debut release.

Billie Eilish built exactly this impressive mark over the last few years. The global phenomenon of When We All Fall Asleep, Where Do We Go? secured the win in the category in 2020. Then, Happier Than Ever returned to the race in 2022, and the acclaimed Hit Me Hard and Soft kept the tradition alive by being included in the 2025 competition.

Olivia Rodrigo's trajectory is following the exact same path of success. Her debut album, Sour, won the award in 2022, while its successor Guts also secured a nomination in the category years later. Now, with the arrival of her latest studio project, the artist has a great chance of seeing her three first albums recognized consecutively by the Recording Academy.

This chronological and artistic coincidence between Billie and Olivia is catching the attention of music experts. Both singers won the main award with their debuts, returned with equally strong albums on their second try, and solidified an expressive streak about five years after making a worldwide impact with their historic debuts.

Big names in music usually accumulate various nominations over decades of career, but almost always stumble on some early release. Established artists like Adele and Ariana Grande had debut albums ignored by the award's voters before becoming frequent figures in the pop categories.

If the new nomination is confirmed for Olivia, she will join a select group of artists who reached the mark of three nominations in the sector, leveling with names like Billie Eilish herself, Adele, and Madonna. Although the total number of nominations for veterans is still higher due to their time in the industry, the initial consistency of Eilish and Rodrigo remains a rare milestone in recent pop history.
