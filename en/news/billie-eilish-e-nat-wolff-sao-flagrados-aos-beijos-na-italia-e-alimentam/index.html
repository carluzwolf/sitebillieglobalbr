Billie Eilish and Nat Wolff spotted kissing in Italy, fueling romance rumors

New photos published by the Deuxmoi account show the singer and the actor in an intimate moment in Venice, reigniting speculation about their relationship.

New reports involving Billie Eilish and actor and musician Nat Wolff stirred up social media and caught the attention of fans around the world. The duo was recently photographed in a romantic moment in the city of Venice, Italy, significantly increasing suspicions that the artists are in a romantic relationship.
