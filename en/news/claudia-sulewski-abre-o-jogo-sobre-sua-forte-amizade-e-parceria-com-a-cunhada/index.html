Claudia Sulewski opens up about her strong friendship and partnership with sister-in-law Billie Eilish

In an exclusive interview, entrepreneur Claudia Sulewski highlighted the harmony and mutual support she shares with Billie Eilish, blending family ties and the beauty business world.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Nov 6, 2026 · 2 min read

The worlds of beauty and entertainment often cross paths in surprising ways, but few partnerships are as genuine as the one built between Claudia Sulewski and pop star Billie Eilish. The two, who already share deep family ties, find a solid foundation in their personal synergy to navigate the challenges of the corporate and artistic worlds together.

For anyone following their journeys, this closeness is no surprise. Claudia, who has a well-established career as an influencer and content creator, expanded her horizons in 2023 by founding Cyklar, her brand focused on body care and fragrances. In the same sector, Billie had already made her mark with great success by launching her first self-titled perfume in 2021, followed by other editions very well received by the public and critics.

The shared experience of developing their own brands has created a unique channel of exchange between the sisters-in-law. Although each maintains the distinct identity of her ventures — Cyklar with its body wellness proposal and Billie's fragrance line with its cruelty-free and striking imprint — behind-the-scenes support is constant. Conversations about the ups and downs of the market have become routine in their family dynamic.

Billie Eilish's success in the fragrance industry did not happen by chance. Since the debut of her perfume Eilish Eau de Parfum, the singer has demonstrated impressive creative involvement, translating her visual and auditory aesthetic into the olfactory realm. The artist has always made it clear that aromas hold special meaning in her life and in her creative synesthesia, quickly winning over fans and the global market.

On the other side, Claudia Sulewski built Cyklar with a keen eye for design and the consumer's sensory experience. The brand reflects the founder's sophisticated yet effortless lifestyle, gaining traction and building a loyal community of engaged customers. It is precisely this passion for creating products from scratch that fuels the synergy between the two businesswomen.

Despite their intense routines of world tours, musical projects, and commercial commitments, Claudia and Billie always find time to strengthen their bond. Fans frequently celebrate moments when the two appear together, whether at public events or in casual social media posts, showing that their friendship bravely withstands the spotlight.

So far, there is no official confirmation regarding a direct collaboration between Cyklar and Billie Eilish's perfume line. However, mutual respect and the exchange of business experiences continue to inspire both the public and new entrepreneurs in the sector. The relationship proves that, at the top of the industry, genuine support among women is the key to lasting success.
