Billie Eilish's past statements about friend's comments resurface on the internet

The singer's old accounts about having been criticized for her own body have recently started circulating on social media again.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Oct 23, 2026 · 2 min read

Billie Eilish's journey in music has always been accompanied by intense public discussions about her physical appearance and clothing choices. Since the beginning of her meteoric rise as a teenager, the artist has had to deal with constant scrutiny from the media and internet users regarding her body.

Recently, new debates took over social media following public appearances and advertising campaigns by the singer, in which followers noticed physical changes. This repercussion led to the circulation of past interviews and statements given by the singer over the years regarding her relationship with her own image.

Among the episodes that started being discussed by fans again, an account shared by the artist in a 2021 interview with Elle magazine stands out. At the time, Billie recalled an uncomfortable moment with a close male friend, who criticized the outfit she was wearing on a video call.

According to the singer, she was wearing a simple tank top when the friend expressed discomfort and asked her to cover up. At the time, the artist pointed out the double standard faced by people with different body types, emphasizing that the same outfit generated different judgments depending on whose body was wearing it.

This type of aesthetic pressure was the main reason why the Grammy winner adopted loose and oversized clothing during her early years in the music industry. The choice acted as a conscious barrier to prevent the public from hypersexualizing her image before people could focus purely on her art and compositions.

Over time, Billie began to explore different visual styles and to open up publicly about the process of self-acceptance. Even with her artistic maturity and the consolidation of her name on the global stage, the debate surrounding her body changes continues to generate strong digital engagement among admirers.

It is worth noting that recent speculations about possible cosmetic procedures and surgeries do not have any official confirmation from the team or the singer herself. Fans and followers remain divided on social media, while the artist continues to focus on her career and new musical ventures.
