Billie Eilish's donation funds planting and guarantees high survival rate of native trees in Texas

A financial contribution linked to the artist's shows allowed the planting of 150 native trees in a green area in the US state.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Nov 4, 2026 · 1 min read

Singer Billie Eilish continues to expand her positive impact far beyond the stage and the music charts. Globally known for her active involvement in socio-environmental issues, the artist directed resources to an ecological revitalization project that recently showed highly promising results on Texan soil.

The initiative gained momentum from a 20,000-dollar financial contribution linked to her tour's stop in the region. The amount was fully applied to the creation of a nearly six-hundred-square-meter grove, specifically designed to receive native plant species of the local ecosystem.

Months after the completion of the initial planting, recent reports indicate that the project achieved an impressive eighty-five percent survival rate among the saplings. This index reflects the technical care in vegetation selection and the proper management of the public green area, which now serves as an ecological and community refuge.

The partnership with sustainability projects has been a trademark of the Happier Than Ever singer's career. In her world tours, such as the acclaimed Hit Me Hard and Soft Tour, the artist frequently implements initiatives that reduce the carbon footprint, promote climate awareness, and support local organizations dedicated to environmental preservation.

Beyond the purely ecological benefit, the creation of the green space in Texas works as an important environmental education tool for local residents. Treed urban areas help mitigate heat island effects, improve air quality, and offer a healthier leisure environment for the public.

To date, local organizers continue to monitor the development of the saplings to ensure the continuous expansion of the grove in the coming years. Concrete actions like this reinforce the positive influence that public figures can exercise when they align their artistic discourses with real practices of planet preservation.
