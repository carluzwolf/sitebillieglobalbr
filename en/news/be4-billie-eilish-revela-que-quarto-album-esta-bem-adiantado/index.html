BE4: Billie Eilish reveals that fourth album is well underway

In a recent interview, the singer confirmed that the project succeeding "HIT ME HARD AND SOFT" has already passed the halfway point of the production process.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Apr 28, 2026 · 2 min read

Fan expectations for Billie Eilish's next musical chapter have just leveled up. The pop star shared encouraging updates about her highly anticipated fourth studio album, a project affectionately being called "BE4" by the fanbase around the world.

Speaking with Elle magazine, the artist opened up about the progress of the recordings and compositions. According to her, the creative process is at a very advanced stage, positively surprising those who closely follow her steps in the music industry.

View post on X/Twitter ↗

Without hiding her enthusiasm, the singer revealed that production has already passed the halfway mark. This confirmation shows that the creative mind behind major global hits never stops, dedicating herself intensely to shaping the successor of her previous works.

The new project comes after the massive cultural and commercial impact of "HIT ME HARD AND SOFT", released in 2024. The previous record further consolidated the creative partnership with her brother and producer Finneas, in addition to generating massive hits on streaming platforms, such as the track "BIRDS OF A FEATHER".

During the same conversation, Billie reflected precisely on the artistic risks taken in the most prominent single of her latest record. She pointed out that betting on more pop sounds represented a big gamble, but celebrated the positive result of this boldness in her discography.

Despite the great news about the progress in the studio, it is important to highlight that the record label and the artist herself have not yet released an official release date or the definitive title of the repertoire. The news was also recently reinforced in direct interactions with followers on social media.

While the official announcement does not arrive, fans of Billie Global BR remain attentive to every move the singer makes. The horizon promises strong emotions for the coming months, consolidating another remarkable era in the star's trajectory.
