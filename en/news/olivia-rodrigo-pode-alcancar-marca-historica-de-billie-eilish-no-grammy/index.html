Olivia Rodrigo could match Billie Eilish's historic Grammy milestone

With her upcoming album, the singer could equal Billie Eilish's rare feat of landing her first three records in the main Grammy category.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Oct 4, 2026 · 2 min read

The music industry is closely following developments surrounding Olivia Rodrigo's next studio project. Speculation indicates that the artist has a real chance of repeating a feat that currently belongs exclusively to Billie Eilish within the recent history of global music's most important awards show.

The scenario in question involves the Album of the Year category at the Grammy Awards. Should the highly anticipated record, provisionally cited as You Seem Pretty Sad for a Girl So in Love, receive an official nomination, Olivia will solidify an impressive streak right at the beginning of her artistic journey.

To understand the dimension of this potential milestone, one must look at the history built by Billie Eilish. The interpreter of *Happier Than Ever* shocked the world by seeing her first three full studio projects — the acclaimed extended EP *When We All Fall Asleep, Where Do We Go?*, the successor *Happier Than Ever*, and the acclaimed *Hit Me Hard and Soft* — nominated in the Recording Academy's top award category.

Billie Eilish set an extremely high standard by dominating the Grammys since her overwhelming debut, collecting dozens of nominations and historic wins. The artist has become one of the youngest and most influential figures to shape the rules and prestige of the awards over recent years.

Now, expectations revolve around Olivia Rodrigo and how her new musical era will be received by Academy voters. The singer has already demonstrated strength at the awards with her previous works, solidifying her name in contemporary pop alongside major icons of today.

To date, there is no official confirmation regarding the definitive tracklist or the exact release date of the album that could yield this historic nomination. The industry and fans await the next official announcements from the record label and the artist herself.

Until the official announcement arrives, the public continues to debate the impact this potential nomination will bring to the charts and music history. If it materializes, the feat will reinforce the strength of the new generation of female artists leading the global recording market.
