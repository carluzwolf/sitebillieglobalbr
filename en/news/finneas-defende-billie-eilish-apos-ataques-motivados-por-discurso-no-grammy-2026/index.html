Finneas defends Billie Eilish after attacks motivated by Grammy 2026 speech

The producer came to his sister's defense on social media following negative reactions and criticism of her political statement during the awards show.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Feb 5, 2026 · 2 min read

The Grammy ceremony is known both for major musical victories and moments of strong political resonance, but the 2026 edition resulted in intense developments for Billie Eilish and her brother and creative partner, Finneas. During the event held on the first Sunday of February, the artist won the Song of the Year award and used her time at the microphone to deliver a harsh message against U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement, in addition to highlighting the issue of indigenous territories.

https://twitter.com/BillieSociety/status/2019145495940108467 ↗

The singer's statement generated immediate division on the internet and in the North American press. While immigrant rights advocates praised the star's courage in using her massive platform to speak about urgent issues, conservative sectors and political commentators launched a wave of attacks and attempts to delegitimize the speech, arguing about the artist's residence and the socioeconomic context of celebrities.

Faced with the flurry of criticism directed at Billie, Finneas used his Threads account to respond to detractors without hesitation. The producer criticized the stance of public figures and conservative commentators who tried to minimize his sister's position, even pushing back against opinion pieces published in the mainstream media that classified artists' political demonstrations as irrelevant.

https://twitter.com/PopCrave/status/2019099250169356659 ↗

The controversy took on new twists when political commentators dug up details about the singer's residence, valued at over three million dollars, claiming the property is located on the ancestral lands of the Tongva indigenous tribe. The argument was used by critics to accuse the Grammy winner of hypocrisy, demanding practical actions regarding immigrant housing.

Amidst the debate generated on social media, a spokesperson for the Tongva tribe officially spoke out about the case. The original peoples of the Los Angeles basin stated that they understand the property's location, emphasizing that they appreciate when public figures help bring visibility to the true history of the country, although they pointed out the desire to be explicitly mentioned in future discussions to strengthen the recognition of their ancestral territory.

https://twitter.com/overrheatedd/status/2018921762617266186 ↗

Finneas's firm stance and the balanced response from the Tongva community brought a new level to the discussion about the role of influential voices in today's society. For fans following the Billie Global BR coverage, the episode reinforces the singer's commitment to social causes, showing that, even in the face of pressure and external attacks, the artist remains steadfast in her convictions alongside her brother and main collaborator.
