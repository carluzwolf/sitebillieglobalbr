Billie Eilish and stars ask Biden to change Thanksgiving tradition

The singer joined other celebrities to request that the president send a pardoned turkey to an animal sanctuary.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Nov 12, 2026 · 2 min read

The singer Billie Eilish has joined forces with other major personalities from the entertainment industry to make a direct appeal to United States President Joe Biden. The group asked for a significant change in one of the country's best-known Thanksgiving holiday traditions: the traditional presidential turkey pardoning ceremony.

The initiative, which also had the support of names like actor Joaquin Phoenix, seeks to transform the final destination of the bird chosen annually. Instead of sending the pardoned animal to university facilities or commercial locations after the event at the White House, the artists requested that the turkey be sent directly to an accredited animal sanctuary.

The claim was organized by animal protection entities, which highlight the importance of guaranteeing a dignified life free of exploitation for the bird that escapes the traditional fate of the commemorative date. Billie Eilish's participation reinforces the artist's long-standing engagement with environmental causes, veganism, and animal rights, agendas that she frequently defends on her social networks and world tours.

At 24 years old, the interpreter of What Was I Made For? uses her enormous global influence to support causes linked to sustainability and animal welfare. This is not the first time the singer has taken a political stance or supported initiatives aimed at conservation and respect for wildlife and farmed animals.

To date, the White House administration has not issued an official response regarding the request sent by the coalition of artists and activist organizations. Long-standing traditions like this one usually undergo public scrutiny every year, but changes to the official protocol depend exclusively on the decision of the US presidency.

While fans await updates on the outcome of this open letter, Billie Eilish remains focused on her musical career and her side projects. The artist continues to be one of the most active voices of her generation, proving that her influence goes far beyond the stage and the charts.
