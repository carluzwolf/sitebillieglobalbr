Billie Eilish surprises fans and adopts a new short, layered haircut

The singer opted for a chin-length look full of movement, marking a new phase in her aesthetic journey.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Oct 22, 2026 · 2 min read

Hair changes in the celebrity universe usually stir up the internet quickly, serving as inspiration for millions of fans around the world. Transforming one's hair is one of the most common ways artists find to signal the end of a cycle and the beginning of a new artistic or personal era.

Recently, singer Billie Eilish caught the public's attention by showing off a completely revamped look. Known for her striking and authentic choices throughout her career, the interpreter of Hit Me Hard and Soft decided to make a radical change and adopt an aesthetic quite different from what the public was used to seeing lately.

According to information released by the press, the artist appeared with chin-length hair, opting for a very layered cut full of movement. The news resonated immediately among admirers, who wasted no time commenting on the star's new hair phase on social media.
