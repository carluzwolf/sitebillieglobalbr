Billie Eilish appears unrecognizable in first behind-the-scenes photos of The Bell Jar

New images show the singer in character as Esther Greenwood on the set of the film adaptation directed by Sarah Polley.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Nov 3, 2026 · 2 min read

The transition of major music icons to cinema usually generates immense expectations, but few recent projects carry as much literary weight as the new cinematic venture starring Billie Eilish. The artist, who has collected dozens of awards in the music industry, is diving headfirst into her first leading role on the big screen.

This week, unprecedented shots taken directly behind the scenes left fans surprised by revealing an impressive visual transformation. Far from her usual aesthetic and the striking outfits she usually wears on stage, the singer appeared completely integrated into the atmosphere of the 1950s.

In images captured during filming in Toronto, Eilish wears a red crewneck sweater combined with a plaid skirt in the same shade. The production visually portrays the essence of the protagonist Esther Greenwood, the central character of the acclaimed world literature classic written by Sylvia Plath.

This work marks the artist's first major prominent role in cinema, expanding her acting career after an appearance in the 2023 series Swarm. The project features direction and a script by award-winning filmmaker Sarah Polley, known for her praised work in critically acclaimed international dramatic feature films.

Originally published in 1963, the plot follows the journey of a talented young woman dealing with suffocating social pressures and a severe decline in her mental health during the 1950s. The work is considered one of the most striking and profound narratives of the last century's literature regarding mental health and gender expectations.

The adaptation's star-studded cast also features heavyweights such as Carey Mulligan, slated to play the protagonist's mother. The large-scale production brings together prominent Hollywood companies, including production company Plan B Entertainment, with a scheduled release for the year 2027.

While the public awaits further news about the film, this dramatic foray consolidates a phase of artistic expansion for the singer, who has also been involved with audiovisual direction. The visible dedication to rehearsals and characterization demonstrates the star's commitment to this new and challenging chapter of her career.
