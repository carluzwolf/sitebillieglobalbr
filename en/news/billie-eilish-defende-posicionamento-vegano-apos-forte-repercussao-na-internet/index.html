Billie Eilish defends vegan stance following heavy online backlash

The singer reinforced her convictions about veganism on social media after receiving criticism for stating that consuming meat is wrong.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · May 8, 2026 · 1 min read

Singer Billie Eilish once again stirred up social media by taking a firm stance on veganism. The American artist recently sparked intense public discussion by publicly declaring that the consumption of animal products is inherently incorrect, which drew a mixed wave of reactions online between defenders and critics of her position.

In response to the negative backlash and questions received from various internet users, the Hit Me Hard and Soft singer-songwriter chose to double down on her arguments. On her digital platforms, she shared a series of high-impact visual records depicting the treatment of animals in the food industry prior to slaughter.

Known for her long-standing activist engagement, the pop star has followed a strictly plant-based diet for several years and frequently uses her massive global visibility to advocate for animal rights. The voice behind What Was I Made For? had already commented on several previous occasions about how the transition to a vegan lifestyle transformed her personal and ethical perception of the world.

Although the debate over food sustainability and animal ethics tends to divide opinions on the internet, public figures engaged in the cause frequently face polarization when addressing the topic. To date, the artist's team has not issued new official statements detailing the episode, and there is no additional information regarding further developments in this discussion.

Billie Eilish's activism encompasses not only her diet, but also partnerships with sustainable fashion brands and climate awareness campaigns. The artist remains focused on her current professional projects and connecting with fans around the world, maintaining her transparent stance on the values she defends in her personal life.
