Billie Eilish and Finneas make historic appearance in special The Simpsons short

The pop star and her brother made an appearance in a special episode of the classic animated show, performing an unforgettable duet with Lisa Simpson.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Apr 19, 2022 · 2 min read

Billie Eilish's journey continues to reach milestones in pop culture and world television. The artist and her brother and main producer, Finneas O'Connell, were confirmed as the newest guest stars to be part of the universe of the classic and long-running animation The Simpsons.

View post on X/Twitter ↗

The news reached the public in the format of an exclusive short film for the Disney+ streaming platform. Entitled "When Billie Met Lisa", the production placed the singer and songwriter side by side with one of television's most beloved characters.

In the plot of the special episode, young Lisa Simpson is looking for a quiet place to practice her saxophone when she ends up being surprised by the duo. In the unexpected encounter, Billie and Finneas welcome the musician and invite her to an improvised studio.

The highlight of the animated collaboration was a musical duet between Billie Eilish and Lisa Simpson. The partnership united the distinctive whispered vocal style of the Oscar-winning artist with the unconditional love for jazz of Homer and Marge's middle daughter.

Before this incursion into Springfield, the singer of Bad Guy had already demonstrated a strong connection with audiovisuals and projects of great aesthetic refinement. Her history includes creating the soundtrack for No Time to Die and the release of the acclaimed concert film Happier Than Ever: A Love Letter to Los Angeles on Disney+.

The appearance on The Simpsons further consolidates the artistic versatility of the O'Connell siblings in global entertainment. Their presence on the show reinforces the duo's status not only as contemporary music phenomena, but also as figures of great relevance in current pop culture.

The short film was part of a series of special initiatives by the animated franchise for streaming, bringing renowned musicians closer to the universe created by Matt Groening. For the singer's fans, the release represented yet another opportunity to follow Billie Eilish's steps in projects of worldwide reach.
