HIT ME HARD AND SOFT: the album that revealed a new artistic depth from Billie Eilish

Released in May 2024, the singer's third studio album blends vulnerability, sound experimentation, and artistic maturity in one of the most remarkable projects of her career.

By Billie Global BR · Nov 2, 2026 · 2 min read

On May 17, 2024, Billie Eilish presented HIT ME HARD AND SOFT to the world, her third studio album. Featuring ten tracks, the project arrived on digital platforms proposing a musical experience that goes beyond individual songs: a journey that invites the audience to listen to the record from beginning to end.

The work explores different emotions, ranging from moments of vulnerability, intensity, passion, and personal conflicts. Musically, Billie moves between alternative pop, electronic elements, and experimental sounds, maintaining the identity that made her music recognizable worldwide.

Once again, Billie worked alongside her brother and musical partner, FINNEAS, responsible for production and songwriting alongside the singer. However, in this project, Billie had an even greater participation in the creative process, getting directly involved in different stages of production and vocal recording.

The result is an album that demonstrates the duo's evolution and presents a carefully constructed sound, with details that connect the ten tracks into a unique experience.

Among the songs, some gained special prominence, such as BIRDS OF A FEATHER, WILDFLOWER, LUNCH, and CHIHIRO. Each one presents its own identity, showing different sides of the artist and expanding the project's sonic possibilities.

The closing track with BLUE also deserves attention: the song brings together references to old compositions by Billie and FINNEAS, creating a connection between different moments of the duo's musical trajectory.

With HIT ME HARD AND SOFT, Billie Eilish reaffirms her artistic freedom and demonstrates that she doesn't need to follow traditional formulas to build a remarkable work. The album represents a new stage of her musical evolution, combining experimentation, feelings, and an intimate production.

More than a collection of songs, the record was conceived as a complete work, in which each track contributes to the final experience.
