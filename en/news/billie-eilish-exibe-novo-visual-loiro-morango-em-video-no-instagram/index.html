Billie Eilish debuts new strawberry blonde look in Instagram video

The pop superstar shared a car video highlighting her layered haircut and a nostalgic soundtrack.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Oct 22, 2026 · 2 min read

Singer Billie Eilish caught fans' attention this Thursday by publishing a new post on her official Instagram account. In the video shared on the social network, the 24-year-old artist appears inside a car enthusiastically showing off the details of her short strawberry blonde hair, displaying the fall of the layers and the new texture of her locks to the public.

View post on Instagram ↗

To accompany the visuals, the global hitmaker chose as a soundtrack an alternative rock track released in 1994 by the band Pavement. Maintaining the discretion she usually adopts in her captions, she chose to include only a single emoji, letting followers fill the comment section with compliments and enthusiastic reactions to the aesthetic novelty.

Although the recent video generated great repercussion, the hair change was presented to the public weeks earlier, in early October. On that occasion, the Grammy winner traded her longer, darker strands for a shoulder-length cut, marked by choppy layers and a side part, marking yet another transition in her visual trajectory.

View post on Instagram ↗

Throughout her career, the songwriter has built a striking history of aesthetic transformations that frequently accompany musical eras or new professional projects. From the days when she sported iconic black hair with neon green roots to the platinum blonde phase that marked the release of the album Happier Than Ever, the young artist has always used her image as a tool for creative expression.

The gradual transition to lighter tones and shorter cuts throughout the year has also been closely followed by fashion and entertainment publications. Alongside musical and aesthetic news, the singer has directed part of her energy toward new artistic horizons, including her starring role in the film adaptation of the work The Bell Jar.

Despite the public's constant curiosity surrounding her personal and artistic life, the singer's team has not released additional information about future studio releases. While admirers wait for news in the musical scene, social networks continue to serve as the main channel of direct interaction between the artist and her global fan base.
