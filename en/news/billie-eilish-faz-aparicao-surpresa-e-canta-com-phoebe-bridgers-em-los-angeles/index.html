Billie Eilish makes surprise appearance and sings with Phoebe Bridgers in Los Angeles

Billie Eilish took the stage at the Intuit Dome for a special guest appearance on Phoebe Bridgers' tour, charming the audience with a fun and themed duet.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Oct 31, 2026 · 1 min read

The opening night of Phoebe Bridgers' run at the Intuit Dome in Los Angeles held an unforgettable surprise for the fans in attendance. Amidst moments of deep emotion and reflection presented by the host, the show gained a touch of pure fun with the triumphant entrance of Billie Eilish.

Known for her long-standing friendship and memorable partnerships in the music scene, Eilish surprised the crowd by appearing dressed in a pink onesie pajama and costumes inspired by the classic story of Peter Pan. The collaboration brought lightness and plenty of energy to the California arena.

The two artists shared vocals on a striking performance of the track Savior Complex. In addition to their vocal chemistry, the duo charmed the audience by dancing across the stage in their themed outfits, creating one of the most talked-about moments of the night and drawing enthusiastic applause from fans.

The show was part of Bridgers' current tour, which has caught the media's attention not only for its acclaimed repertoire, but also for a strict rule: the prohibition of cell phones and smart devices in the audience, which are locked in special pouches during the event to guarantee a more immersive experience.

With the absence of screens illuminating the darkness of the arena, the connection between the public and the artists on stage became even more palpable. Billie Eilish's presence crowned the night as one of the great encounters in recent pop and alternative music, reinforcing the complicity between the stars.
