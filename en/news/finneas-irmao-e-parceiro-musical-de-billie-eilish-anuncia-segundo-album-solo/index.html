Finneas, Billie Eilish's brother and musical partner, announces second solo album

The producer and singer revealed details for For Cryin' Out Loud!, his new studio project arriving on streaming platforms in October 2024.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Aug 9, 2024 · 2 min read

To the delight of fans who closely follow the Eilish family, the talented singer, songwriter, and producer Finneas has taken an important step in his solo career. At 27 years old, the artist officially announced the arrival of his second studio album, titled For Cryin' Out Loud!, scheduled for October 4, 2024.

The new project follows his acclaimed debut record Optimist, released in 2021. Since then, Finneas's creative routine has been intensely divided between his individual work and his iconic partnership with his sister, Billie Eilish, with whom he has shaped global hits and the recent acclaimed album Hit Me Hard and Soft.

View post on Instagram ↗

In addition to producing the entire repertoire of For Cryin' Out Loud!, Finneas took advantage of the announcement to treat the public to the music video for the title track. The complete tracklist was also revealed, featuring brand new compositions that have sparked curiosity, such as Starfucker, Sweet Cherries, and Family Feud.

The artist has accumulated several major music industry awards alongside his sister, including Grammy statuettes. Among his most recent achievements are victories at the 2024 ceremony in the categories of Song of the Year and Best Song Written for Visual Media, thanks to the megahit What Was I Made For?, the official soundtrack of the cinematic phenomenon Barbie.

In a recent interview with Rolling Stone magazine, Finneas reflected on current consumption habits in the digital industry. The musician commented on how young audiences often prioritize quick snippets that go viral on social media over listening to complete bodies of work, but expressed a lot of optimism about the future.

For the producer, the current landscape acts as a passing trend. He believes that the music market will experience a natural counterculture, where listeners will eventually return to the habit of immersing themselves in entire albums to seek deeper artistic connections.

Until October 4 arrives, fans of the Eilish family can check out the content released by the artist on his official social media accounts. In the Billie Global BR community, we continue to closely follow every step of this new solo era from our favorite producer.
