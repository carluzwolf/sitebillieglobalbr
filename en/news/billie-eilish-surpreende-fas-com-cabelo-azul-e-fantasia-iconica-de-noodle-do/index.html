Billie Eilish Surprises Fans with Blue Hair and Iconic Noodle from Gorillaz Costume

The singer shared an unprecedented look for Halloween on her social media, opting for blue locks and a latex dress.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Nov 5, 2026 · 2 min read

Billie Eilish's creativity once again caught the attention of the international public during Halloween celebrations. Known for her striking visual transformations and authentic aesthetic choices, the Grammy-winning artist used her official Instagram profile to reveal the outfit chosen for the festive date.

This time, the *Happier Than Ever* interpreter surprised followers by adopting vibrant blue hair. The new look consisted of a layered bob cut with bangs, directly recalling the visual style of the character Noodle from the famous virtual band Gorillaz.

In addition to the hair change that drew significant attention, the star composed the outfit with a tight minilength latex dress. The choice reinforces the singer's affinity with pop culture and animation references, paying a creative tribute to one of the most influential musical projects of recent decades.

The publication quickly accumulated millions of likes and generated enthusiastic debates among fans on social media, who praised the artist's versatility and dedication to the details of the characterization. Although the blue hair was part of the festive production, the novelty sparked speculation about possible future aesthetic phases for the singer.

Throughout her career, Billie Eilish has transitioned through several memorable visual identities, ranging from the classic black hair with neon green roots to the platinum blonde of the *Happier Than Ever* era and the more recent dark tones. Each change usually marks new artistic moments in the young star's trajectory.

To date, there is no official confirmation from the team or the artist herself whether the blue coloring is here to stay or if it was just a temporary trick for the Halloween celebration. Either way, the repercussion once again proves the global impact of every step the singer takes.
