Finneas, Billie Eilish's musical partner, celebrates Lula's victory and sees hope for the United States

The North American producer and musician reacted to the reelection in Brazil and highlighted that American politics could follow the same path.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Oct 26, 2026 · 2 min read

The international political landscape once again attracted the attention of big names in the music industry this week. Finneas O'Connell, renowned producer, singer, and Billie Eilish's main creative partner, used his social media to react to the reelection of President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva in Brazil. The artist's statement quickly caught the attention of fans around the world, especially due to the parallel drawn with the political context of the United States.

Known both for his work in building Billie Eilish's intimate and award-winning sound and for his firm public stances, Finneas used his digital platform to share an analysis of the Brazilian election. The musician reposted a politically charged message that celebrated the outcome at the polls in the South American country, interpreting the result as a significant milestone against global conservative trends.

The publication shared by the producer highlighted that the electoral victory in Brazil represented a hard blow against far-right figures and against the influence exerted by large technology companies in politics. The message strongly resonated with the artist, who frequently uses his visibility to comment on social issues and encourage the civic participation of his North American followers.

In the same vein, Finneas made a direct observation about the political future of his own country. The musician suggested that the Brazilian example serves as inspiration and proof that expressive changes are possible, stating that America can also achieve the same result if it follows the mobilization seen here. The statement generated widespread repercussion among internet users and admirers of his work with Billie Eilish.

This is not the first time that the background of the Eilish family intersects with discussions of global relevance. Although Billie and Finneas keep their main focus on their musical careers, marked by sold-out world tours and multiple Grammy awards, both usually use their voices to address themes of social justice, the environment, and democracy. The connection with the Brazilian public, always very present and passionate on social media, gained an extra chapter with this demonstration of interest and optimism from the producer regarding the country's direction.
