Billie Eilish gets grove of trees in Austin thanks to sustainability initiative

A grove with 150 native trees was informally named in honor of the singer in Austin, as a result of donations from her tour.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Oct 30, 2026 · 2 min read

Billie Eilish's tour stop in Austin, Texas, left a permanent and green legacy in the North American city. The artist received an informally named grove of trees in her honor, located in one of the region's most traditional green areas, Zilker Park. The space of about 550 square meters is strategically positioned along a walking trail, just south of the famous Barton Springs pool.

The project was made possible through a generous donation of twenty thousand dollars, funded by the singer's ticket program dedicated to socio-environmental causes. The initiative was tied to the successful Hit Me Hard and Soft tour, which raised millions of dollars globally to support ecological and social justice projects. With the funds, the location received one hundred and fifty saplings of native species from the region.

Among the varieties planted are trees such as flowering dogwoods, mountain laurels, Mexican plums, and redbuds, which help compose the local landscape. The planting process relied on strong mobilization from the community and environmental activists. More than one hundred and fifty volunteers dedicated their time over two weekends in March to get their hands dirty and structure the new grove.

More than just beautifying the landscape, the space has a vital ecological function for the Austin region. The chosen area sits just above a portion of the Edwards Aquifer, an important underground water source responsible for supplying drinking water to about 600,000 people. The landscaping project included raised soil barriers and shallow channels specifically designed to slow down and filter polluted rainwater before it reaches Barton Creek.

The development of the ecosystem has shown very promising results so far. The planted saplings record an impressive survival rate of eighty-five percent, demonstrating the effectiveness of the management. Furthermore, the project was recently expanded with the planting of a wildflower meadow nearby, further increasing local biodiversity.

The new grove is now fully open for public visitation, attracting fans of the singer and nature enthusiasts. Local authorities stress that visitors must stay strictly on the marked trails to ensure the protection and healthy growth of the young trees. The tribute further consolidates Billie Eilish's public commitment to sustainability and environmental preservation wherever she goes.
