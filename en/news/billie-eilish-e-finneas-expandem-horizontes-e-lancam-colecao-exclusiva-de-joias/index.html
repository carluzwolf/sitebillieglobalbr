Billie Eilish and Finneas expand their horizons and launch an exclusive jewelry collection in partnership with John Hardy

The singer and her brother surprised fans by announcing an unprecedented collaboration in the world of high jewelry, uniting delicacy and alternative style.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Oct 21, 2026 · 2 min read

The creativity of Billie Eilish and Finneas seems to know no bounds. Known worldwide for revolutionizing contemporary pop music with acclaimed albums, sold-out tours, and multiple Grammy awards, the siblings now officially add the title of jewelry designers to their impressive artistic resume. The news was revealed to the public this week, generating great repercussion among the duo's admirers and fashion enthusiasts.

For this unprecedented endeavor, the duo joined forces with the renowned brand John Hardy. The result of this collaboration is a collection that perfectly translates the artists' unique visual identity, balancing delicate elements with a striking and alternative aesthetic. Each piece reflects the siblings' personal taste for accessories that stray from the ordinary, while maintaining the characteristic sophistication of the partner brand.

Throughout their careers, Billie and Finneas have always used fashion as a powerful extension of their artistic expressions. From the days of oversized outfits and eccentric looks to the more recent red carpet phases, the singer's style is constantly copied and celebrated globally. Their official entry into jewelry design crowns this long-standing relationship with the fashion universe, allowing fans to acquire items that carry the duo's aesthetic essence.

John Hardy is widely recognized for its commitment to sustainable craftsmanship and the use of high-quality materials in jewelry production. This ecological and ethical philosophy aligns directly with the environmental issues that Billie Eilish fervently defends in her personal life and on her world tours. The choice of the brand for this design debut demonstrates the siblings' care in associating their names with projects that respect socio-environmental values.

Although the announcement has excited the public, full details regarding the global availability of the products, price ranges, and points of sale are still being gradually released by the brand's and the artists' official channels. Fans wishing to acquire the pieces should keep an eye on international updates, as the brand typically has selective distribution in global markets.

This news arrives at a very busy time for the duo, who continue to reap the rewards of their recent musical projects and their strong presence in pop culture. Whether breaking records on the music charts or innovating in the accessories market, Billie and Finneas prove once again that they are creative powerhouses capable of moving through different industries with authenticity and success.
