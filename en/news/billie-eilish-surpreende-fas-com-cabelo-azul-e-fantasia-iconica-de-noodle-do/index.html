Billie Eilish Surprises Fans with Blue Hair and Iconic Noodle from Gorillaz Costume

Billie Eilish's creativity once again caught the attention of the international public during Halloween celebrations. Throughout her career, Billie Eilish has transitioned through several memorable visual identities, ranging from the classic black hair with neon green roots to the platinum blonde of the *Happier Than Ever* era and the more recent dark tones. Each change usually marks new artistic moments in the young star's trajectory.
