Billie Eilish and Finneas launch sustainable jewelry collection in partnership with John Hardy

After taking on the role of ambassadors, the siblings created an exclusive ten-piece line focused on recycled metals and lab-grown diamonds.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Oct 22, 2026 · 2 min read

Billie Eilish and her brother and musical partner, Finneas O’Connell, expanded their creativity beyond the studios and stages by signing a new jewelry collection in collaboration with the traditional brand John Hardy. The project, recently revealed, took about two years of meticulous development to reflect the aesthetic identity and particular tastes of the duo of artists.

The relationship between the musicians and the brand began to grow closer in September 2025, when they officially assumed the role of ambassadors for the Lovestruck line. From the beginning, the shared environmental commitment served as the main bridge for the union, aligning with the well-known ecological positions that Billie and Finneas defend in their careers and personal lives.

For this limited edition, the focus on sustainability translated into the expressive use of conscious materials. More than 99% of the metals used in the ten sterling silver pieces—which include necklaces, rings, bracelets, and earrings—are fully recovered, reinforcing the circular economy proposal valued by both parties.

The collection was divided into two distinct creative branches: "Wildflower" and "Screwball". The first relies on delicate floral themes adorned with lab-grown diamonds, directly dialoguing with the aesthetic preferences that the singer usually adopts in her daily routine away from the big spotlights.

In contrast, the "Screwball" line explores chains with an industrial and robust appeal, referring to the energetic look that usually marks the singer's live performances. The curation of the items sought to translate different moments and forms of expression through striking accessories.

According to statements released during the launch, jewelry has always occupied a prominent space in the construction of the artist's self-expression. The project made it possible to materialize their appreciation for minimalist pendants and elements of nature, resulting in a versatile catalog aligned with the ethical values of the current generation of fans.
