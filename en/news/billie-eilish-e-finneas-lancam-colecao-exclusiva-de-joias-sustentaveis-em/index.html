Billie Eilish and Finneas launch exclusive sustainable jewelry collection in partnership with John Hardy

The siblings have joined forces with the renowned brand to create artisanal pieces that reflect their unique aesthetic and environmental commitment.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Oct 21, 2026 · 2 min read

The creativity of siblings Billie Eilish and Finneas O'Connell has just expanded into the universe of fine jewelry. The duo officially announced the launch of their first jewelry line in collaboration with the traditional brand John Hardy, a project that unites the artists' striking design with the artisanal expertise of the international luxury brand.

Named The Billie Eilish & Finneas Collection, the complete line is now available for purchase through the brand's official website. Fans and fashion enthusiasts can acquire the items with prices ranging from 195 to 2,400 dollars, covering different price ranges and varied options of exclusive accessories.

Known for their strong convictions regarding sustainability and ecological responsibility, Billie and Finneas made a point for the partnership to reflect these fundamental values. John Hardy is widely recognized in the market for using ethical production practices, employing recycled metals and responsibly sourced stones in their artisanal creations.

This is not the first time that the musicians' aesthetic overflows into the fashion and lifestyle market. Throughout their meteoric career, which began with the global success of their debut album When We All Fall Asleep, Where Do We Go?, Billie Eilish has dictated visual trends, always prioritizing comfort and an authentic identity.

In turn, Finneas also imprints his refined aesthetic taste both in music production and in his visual choices, perfectly complementing his sister's artistic vision. The creative union of the two in the studio now translates into forms sculpted in metal, bringing the duo's essence to pieces that can be worn on a daily basis.

To date, details about future expansions of the line to selected physical stores have not been detailed by the brand's press office. However, digital availability has already mobilized fans around world who wish to collect yet another official item signed by the Oscar and Grammy-winning artists.

For the Brazilian and international public that follows every step of the siblings' trajectory, the collection represents another bridge between avant-garde music and the universe of conscious design. With this venture, Billie Eilish and Finneas continue to expand their creative horizons far beyond the stages and the hit charts.
