Billie Eilish surprises fans by adopting strawberry red look with new bob cut

The Grammy-winning singer shared her new hair transformation on social media, showing off reddish locks.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Oct 27, 2026 · 2 min read

The pop music chameleon has a new look. Singer Billie Eilish caught fans' attention by showing off her latest haircut, known as a bob, in a strawberry red shade that quickly went viral on social media. The artist, known as much for her musical innovation as for her striking aesthetic choices, has taken another step in her constant visual evolution.

Known for transitioning between different color palettes throughout her career, the voice behind the album Hit Me Hard and Soft shared the news through a video posted on her official Instagram account. In the recording, taken in the front seat of a car, she moves her head from side to side to show off the cut and the new coloring to her followers, accompanying the post with the soundtrack of the classic song Cut Your Hair by the band Pavement.

The first public appearance of this hair phase happened earlier in the month, when the star shared a mirror selfie that had already raised suspicions among more attentive admirers. Now, with the new video, the change is solidified as the official strawberry bob era for the artist, who continues to promote her most recent works in the music industry.

Throughout her successful career, the ten-time Grammy winner has marked generations with iconic hair transformations. It is impossible to forget the phase when she sported neon-green roots with black hair, the trademark of the launch of her acclaimed debut album, When We All Fall Asleep, Where Do We Go?. Years later, the singer surprised the world by adopting the platinum blonde that marked the era of her second studio album, Happier Than Ever.

Billie's color palette has also featured shades of blue, silver, dark brown, and even a vibrant red, proving that she is not afraid to dare and reinvent her image with each artistic cycle. Each visual change is usually accompanied by new chapters in the singer's career, whether in the release of musical projects, the expansion of her perfume brand, or at major award shows.

While enjoying the continued success of her current tour and the impact of her recent releases, the artist continues to influence global style and behavior trends. The warm reception to the new look once again demonstrates the strength of the connection between the singer and her millions of fans around the world, who eagerly follow every step of her artistic journey.
