Billie Eilish shocks fans by debuting short haircut and copper tone

The artist surprised the public this Thursday (22) by showing off a new Italian bob look in a strawberry blonde shade on social media.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Oct 22, 2026 · 2 min read

Singer Billie Eilish stirred up social media on Thursday morning (22) by posting an unprecedented video on her Instagram profile. In the recording, the pop star revealed a radical change in her appearance, showing much shorter and revamped locks that quickly became a trending topic among followers worldwide.

The new style adopted by the singer is a classic cut known as the Italian bob, characterized by structured layers and a chin-length cut. In addition to the shape, the coloration also drew a lot of attention, opting for the strawberry blonde shade, a copper blonde that combines golden and reddish nuances.

In the shared clip, Eilish appears seated in a car, positioning the camera strategically to highlight the details of the transformation and the fall of her new hair. The choice of the video's soundtrack brought a touch of humor and irony, being backed by the song Cut Your Hair by alternative rock band Pavement.

The reaction to the post was immediate on digital platforms, with thousands of admirers commenting on the artist's boldness. The harmony between the new look and the singer's striking aesthetic identity was widely praised by Brazilian and international fans, who approved of the novelty.

Known for her striking visual transformations throughout her career, Billie Eilish has already gone through several memorable aesthetic phases. From her iconic black hair with fluorescent green roots to the platinum blonde of the Happier Than Ever era, passing through recent dark and red tones, the artist frequently uses her image as an extension of her artistic expression.

So far, there is no official confirmation whether the new haircut is part of the preparation for a new musical era or specific visual project. While admirers wait for news about the next steps in the singer's career, it remains to follow social media to check out the developments of this hair phase.
