Billie Eilish surprises fans with flawless Noodle from Gorillaz costume on Halloween

The singer shared snapshots of her Halloween celebration featuring a look inspired by the virtual guitarist of the band Gorillaz, highlighting her strong connection with Damon Albarn.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Nov 5, 2026 · 2 min read

Billie Eilish's creativity during Halloween festivities always generates great anticipation among fans around the world. In November 2026, the pop star continued the tradition that admirers affectionately nicknamed Billieween, showing off a polished production full of musical references on social media.

This time, the artist's choice was to pay homage to one of her favorite alternative bands from her adolescence: Gorillaz. Billie recreated the iconic look of virtual guitarist Noodle, betting on faithful details that quickly won over followers on social media.

The complete costume featured a short electric blue wig, a red and white latex tube dress, as well as striped socks, white gloves, and customized sneakers. Among the published snapshots, the singer appeared having fun excitedly at the party, although in one of the images she resorted to a strategic blue star emoji on the very short dress to avoid a minor wardrobe malfunction.

Billie's relationship with Damon Albarn's project is no secret to the public, as the artist frequently mentions the group's influence on her artistic upbringing. In past performances, such as at the Coachella festival, she even shared the stage with the vocalist, praising the impact his music had on her career.

In the past, Albarn publicly commented on the possibility of a studio collaboration, although plans have not fully materialized so far. While a joint unreleased track is not released, admirers continue to celebrate the musical references and passion that the singer makes a point of demonstrating.

The post quickly accumulated thousands of comments praising the aesthetic adopted for the commemorative date and praising the artist's versatility. For Brazilian fans of Billie Global BR, the moment reinforces how visual identity and good humor continue to mark the best moments of the star's career.
