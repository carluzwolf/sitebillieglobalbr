Remembering Happier Than Ever, Billie Eilish's acclaimed second album

Released in 2021, Billie Eilish's second album consolidated the artist globally by mixing intimate confessions, artistic maturity, and innovative sounds.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Jul 29, 2021 · 2 min read

When she burst onto the international music scene with her acclaimed debut album When We All Fall Asleep, Where Do We Go?, American singer-songwriter Billie Eilish quickly conquered the world, transforming into one of the music industry's biggest phenomena.

After reaching the top of the charts and accumulating historic Grammy trophies, the young artist faced the abrupt challenges of early fame, dealing with public scrutiny, loneliness, and the transition into adulthood. Instead of hiding from these pains, Eilish channeled all these complex experiences into the creation of her eagerly awaited second studio project.

Composed of sixteen unreleased tracks, the album functioned as a true intimate diary, revealing an even more mature and vulnerable facet of the musician at nineteen years of age. Alongside her brother and trusted producer, Finneas O'Connell, the star built a sophisticated sound that defied the traditional formulas of commercial pop at the time.

The opening of the repertoire, marked by the track Getting Older, establishes the project's confessional tone by addressing maturation and the scars of the past in a visceral way. The sonic journey transitions through different musical genres with impressive fluidity, including passages of minimalist R&B, flirtations with bossa nova, and intense electronic beats.

Among the highlights of the tracklist, tracks like Your Power and the intense title track won over both the public and specialized critics for their lyrical depth and surprising arrangements. While the former bets on a delicate acoustic atmosphere about abuse of power, the latter explodes into energetic guitars in its final stretch, showcasing the interpreter's vocal versatility.

The project's commercial and critical success confirmed Billie Eilish's singular talent for constantly reinventing herself without losing her authentic essence. The album not only reaffirmed her prominent place in contemporary music, but also paved the way for sold-out world tours and new consecrated musical eras in the following years.
