Billie Eilish reveals curious detail about the opening of her shows on the Hit Me Hard and Soft tour

In a recent interview, the singer shared curiosities about the enigmatic start of her current performances and what makes this experience liberating.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Dec 8, 2024 · 2 min read

The Hit Me Hard and Soft world tour has provided unforgettable moments for both the audience and Billie Eilish herself. Since the release of her third studio album, the artist has been traveling the globe with a visually impressive production that redefines the dynamic between the alternative pop star and her fans in large arenas.

One of the most talked-about points by fans on social media happens in the very first minutes of the show, when a giant light box rises on stage. During a recent conversation with the press, the singer decided to open up and joked about the mystery surrounding this scenic element right at the start of the show.

With her usual good humor, Billie explained that the object works as a strategic hiding place that instigates the audience's curiosity. She revealed that she is effectively inside the luminous cube at the moment it rises, even though the public cannot see her silhouette at that exact instant of the performance.

The *Birds of a Feather* singer detailed that the transparent structure allows for a privileged view of everything happening around her. From inside the light box, she can perfectly see every person present in the arena, creating a curious dynamic of mutual observation before even singing the first note.

This temporary anonymity was described by the star as a deeply liberating feeling. Being able to analyze the crowd's energy and see the expectation on the fans' faces without being under immediate visual scrutiny completely changes the psychological preparation for facing thousands of people every night.

The Hit Me Hard and Soft era has been marked by this constant search for authenticity and direct connection, maintaining the intimate essence that established the artist, even on gigantic stages. The tour continues to gather praise from specialized critics and sell out arenas around the world, consolidating the current phase of Billie Eilish's career.
