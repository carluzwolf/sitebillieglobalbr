Billie Eilish featured in article on veganism and sustainability

The singer was mentioned in a national article discussing the vegan lifestyle adopted by celebrities and essential health care.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Oct 30, 2026 · 2 min read

The O Globo portal recently published a special feature on veganism, highlighting international and national public figures who have decided to eliminate the consumption of animal products from their routines. Among the high-profile names cited in the text is Billie Eilish, globally recognized for using her immense platform to advocate for environmental causes and animal welfare.

Since her teens, the voice behind global hits has embraced the cruelty-free lifestyle, becoming one of the most influential voices of her generation when it comes to sustainability. The artist frequently uses her social media and public appearances to raise fans' awareness about the impacts of the livestock industry on the planet, encouraging more conscious choices aligned with environmental preservation.

The article also points out that the movement goes far beyond the plate, encompassing conscious consumption and ecological concern. In addition to Billie, the report listed Brazilian personalities such as Xuxa Meneghel, Yasmin Brunet, and Luisa Mell, who also use their visibility to advocate for animal rights and the expansion of an animal exploitation-free routine.

To deepen the debate on health impacts, the outlet consulted nutrition experts to clarify common doubts about this dietary transition. It was emphasized that adopting a plant-based menu requires proper planning and food variety, demystifying the idea that the absence of meat, by itself, automatically guarantees a healthy routine.

Among the main points highlighted by the professionals is the importance of ensuring the correct intake of proteins through legumes, grains, and soy derivatives. Another fundamental warning made in the report concerns vitamin B12, an essential nutrient that is not naturally found in sufficient quantities in vegetables, making supplementation and regular professional monitoring indispensable.

Finally, the text reinforces that veganism should not be strictly viewed as a miraculous method for weight loss, since ultra-processed products can also be part of this universe. For Billie Eilish fans and admirers of the cause, the article serves as an encouragement to seek quality information, respect the individuality of one's own body, and adopt sustainable practices responsibly.
