Billie Eilish and Finneas launch exclusive jewelry collection in partnership with John Hardy

The Grammy-winning siblings joined forces with the John Hardy brand to create a jewelry line split between delicate style and an industrial look.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Oct 21, 2026 · 2 min read

The creativity of siblings Billie Eilish and Finneas has crossed the boundaries of the music industry and expanded into the universe of fine jewelry. Known worldwide for accumulating major Grammy awards and making their mark on generations with their releases, the duo surprised fans by announcing an unprecedented design collaboration.

The artists teamed up with the renowned brand John Hardy to develop an exclusive collection of accessories that directly reflects their contrasting personalities and aesthetic preferences. The project was officially launched on October 21st, becoming available both in selected physical stores and on the brand's official website.

Divided into two main branches, the catalog seeks to balance softness with boldness. The first line was named Wildflower, a clear reference to the track of the same name featured on the acclaimed album Hit Me Hard and Soft. This selection focuses on sterling silver rings, earrings, and necklaces adorned with lab-grown diamonds and floral pendants.

In contrast, the second line was named Screwball and directs its focus toward a heavier, urban look. Composed of thick chains, sculptural bracelets, and industrial-inspired hardware, this part of the collection dialogues perfectly with the striking aesthetic that Eilish usually displays on stage.

According to information released about the project, the musicians' involvement was deep and enduring. Billie and Finneas dedicated an entire year of work alongside the brand's design teams in New York and Bali, closely following every step, from conceptual sketches to the finishing of the pieces.

Sustainability was also at the center of the creative process, with the use of recycled metals and lab-grown stones. The curation caters to both more discreet everyday moments and the robust energy required on world tours, as highlighted by the singer herself in an official statement about the partnership.
