Billie Eilish and Nat Wolff spotted together in a romantic moment in Italy

The break from public exposure has come to an end for Billie Eilish and Nat Wolff. The couple, who had been keeping their relationship out of the spotlight in recent months, was recently spotted enjoying a romantic trip across Europe, surprising fans with spontaneous appearances.
