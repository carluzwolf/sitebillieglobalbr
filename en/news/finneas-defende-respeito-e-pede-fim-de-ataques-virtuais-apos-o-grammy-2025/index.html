Finneas defends respect and asks for an end to online attacks after the 2025 Grammys

The producer and songwriter used social media to congratulate the award winners and asked fans to avoid fights on the internet.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Feb 3, 2025 · 2 min read

The 67th Grammy Awards, held in Los Angeles, caused a stir on the internet and mobilized the international music community. Among the biggest names of the night were Billie Eilish and her brother and longtime producer, Finneas, who closely followed all the categories they were nominated in following the release of the acclaimed album HIT ME HARD AND SOFT.

Despite the enormous expectations from the public and specialized critics, the night ended without wins for the duo in the seven categories they were competing in for trophies. Among the missed nominations were heavy-hitting races, such as Record of the Year, Album of the Year, and Song of the Year, as well as specific performance and pop categories.

Even with the result that generated frustration among part of the fandom, the stance adopted by the artists was one of total maturity and public elegance. Right after the ceremony ended, Finneas took to his official Instagram account to speak out about the experience of attending yet another Recording Academy event.

In his message on the platform, the musician made a point of celebrating the nomination alongside so many prominent works recently released in the music industry. He emphasized the honor of having his and his sister's names considered among the best projects of the period evaluated by the awards.

View post on X/Twitter ↗

Following the post, the producer directed a direct message to fans who used images of the two on their social media profiles. He firmly asked that no one start arguments or conflicts with followers of other artists in the internet comment sections, reinforcing an appeal for harmony.

Billie Eilish's trajectory at the awards continues to be one of the most impressive in recent music history, totaling dozens of nominations and nine golden gramophones won since her sensational debut in 2020. So far, there is no official confirmation of further statements from the singer regarding the results of the latest ceremony.
