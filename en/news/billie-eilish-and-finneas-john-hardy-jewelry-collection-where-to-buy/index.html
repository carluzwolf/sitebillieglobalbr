Billie Eilish and Finneas launch exclusive sustainable jewelry collection in partnership with John Hardy

The siblings expand their creative horizons with an unprecedented line of handmade jewelry developed in collaboration with the luxury brand.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Oct 21, 2026 · 2 min read

The creativity of Billie Eilish and her brother and producer, Finneas O'Connell, has always transcended the barriers of the music industry. Known worldwide for shaping the aesthetics of alternative pop music with acclaimed albums and sold-out tours, the two have now decided to explore entirely new territory by joining forces with renowned sustainable jewelry brand John Hardy for the launch of an exclusive collaborative collection.

Available to the public starting October 21, 2026, the accessories line materializes the duo's striking artistic vision into metal and gemstone pieces. The initiative reflects the artists' history of associating their image with projects that prioritize environmental responsibility, aligning with the sustainability standards and ethical practices championed by the jewelry house.

Although the siblings' creative ecosystem is widely associated with audiovisual productions and iconic stage outfits, this endeavor marks an unprecedented step in their foray into haute couture design. Fans who have followed the singer's journey since the era of When We All Fall Asleep, Where Do We Go? recognize her appreciation for statement accessories, robust rings, and chains that have always made up her unmistakable looks.

John Hardy is widely recognized in the global luxury market for using 100% recycled silver and ethically sourced gold in its handcrafted creations in Indonesia. The choice of the brand for this special partnership with the musicians was no coincidence, as it shares fundamental values advocated by Billie and Finneas regarding planet preservation and conscious consumption.

Specific details about the design of each piece, the exact materials used, and selected physical points of sale have begun to be revealed through the official social media channels of the brand and the artists. So far, information regarding potential limited editions or plans for future expansion of the line has not been officially confirmed by the duo's representatives.

For Brazilian fans wishing to acquire the items, the recommendation is to follow John Hardy's official e-commerce channels and updates from the Billie Global BR portal. The collab solidifies yet another chapter in the siblings' multifaceted career, proving that their influence dictates trends not only in headphones, but also in global fashion.
