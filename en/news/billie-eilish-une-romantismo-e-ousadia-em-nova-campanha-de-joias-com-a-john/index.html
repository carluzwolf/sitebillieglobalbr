Billie Eilish blends romanticism and boldness in new jewelry campaign with John Hardy

Alongside her brother Finneas, the singer debuted a fine jewelry collaboration that contrasts delicate lace pieces with heavy metallic accessories.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Oct 21, 2026 · 2 min read

Billie Eilish continues to shape the course of contemporary fashion and has further cemented her status as a global style icon by announcing a new venture in the world of fine jewelry. The Oscar-winning star joined creative forces with the renowned atelier John Hardy to launch an exclusive collaborative collection, starring in the official campaign alongside her brother and longtime musical partner, Finneas.

For the promotional photos recently released, the artist adopted an aesthetic that transitions seamlessly between vintage romanticism and the urban attitude she is known for. She appeared wearing a delicate ivory-toned satin and lace tank top, held up only by thin spaghetti straps. The boudoir-inspired piece also featured a romantic bow at the waist, creating a soft silhouette that dialogues with the brand's proposition.

However, the main highlight of the look was the intentional contrast provided by the jewelry. The singer layered over the delicacy of the fabric a robust selection of sterling silver accessories from the collaborative collection itself. The styling featured thick chain-link necklaces around the neck, striking bracelets, and textured rings that showcase the fusion between industrial elements and floral motifs characteristic of the line.

Finneas also integrated the campaign's visual records, posing alongside his sister in a patterned button-down shirt and silver chains in harmony with the aesthetic proposal. In one of the main shots, the duo appears in an intimate pose, while the singer displays her dark hair in soft waves and a natural makeup with an illuminated finish.

This new partnership reinforces the artist's ability to dictate trends without following conventional standards, blending references from different aesthetic universes with total authenticity. Whether on stage or in major global advertising campaigns, she demonstrates that she understands luxury and behavior like few names of her generation.

Brazilian fans and fashion admirers around the world are already celebrating yet another successful step in the singer's fashion career. More details about the availability of the pieces and the collection's developments can be followed directly on the brand's official channels and through updates on the Billie Global BR portal.
