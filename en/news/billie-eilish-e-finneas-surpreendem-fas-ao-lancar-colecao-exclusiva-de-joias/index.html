Billie Eilish and Finneas surprise fans by launching an exclusive sustainable floral jewelry collection

The iconic singer and her brother have joined forces with renowned brand John Hardy to create handcrafted silver pieces inspired by nature.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Oct 23, 2026 · 2 min read

The creativity of Billie Eilish and her brother and producer, Finneas, seems to have no limits when it comes to exploring new forms of artistic expression. This time, the Grammy-winning duo decided to expand their creative talent into the universe of fine jewelry, surprising fans around the world with the launch of an unprecedented jewelry collection in partnership with the traditional brand John Hardy.

:Known worldwide for its ethical manufacturing practices and use of recycled metals, the fashion house found the perfect partnership for the project in the siblings' striking aesthetic. The line is composed primarily of hand-crafted silver pieces, featuring the shape of flowers as a central element, a design that directly dialogues with the most recent and organic visual phase of the artist's discography.

:Historically, Billie's style has always fluctuated between oversized streetwear and statement accessories, while Finneas tends to maintain a minimalist and sophisticated stance in his visual compositions. The union of these two worlds resulted in versatile ornaments that reflect both the singer's strong personality and the producer's refined taste, creating a unique aesthetic balance for the audience that follows both of their careers.

:Beyond the aesthetic appeal, the project reinforces the long-standing commitment that the siblings maintain to sustainability and environmental preservation. John Hardy is widely respected in the global market for using 100% reclaimed silver and gold, as well as supporting reforestation initiatives in Bali, where its main workshop is located. This synergy of values was fundamental for the collaboration to move from concept to reality.

:While fans eagerly await new updates regarding possible extensions of the world tour promoting the acclaimed album Hit Me Hard and Soft, the arrival of this accessories line offers a new way to connect with the visual identity of the current era. Pieces like rings, necklaces, and detailed bracelets are already starting to catch the attention of fashion and music enthusiasts.

:To date, the brand and the artists have not officially disclosed detailed information about international shipping availability or the exact prices of each item. Fans must keep an eye on the official channels of the brand and the singer to find out when the products will be accessible to international buyers.
