Billie Eilish surprises fans by dressing up as Gorillaz's Noodle for Halloween costume

The singer opted for an exclusive VEX latex look to recreate the virtual band's iconic character on Halloween.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Nov 9, 2026 · 2 min read

Billie Eilish once again caught the public's attention during Halloween celebrations. The artist shared the details of her costume for the date on social media, choosing to pay tribute to one of alternative music's most striking names by transforming into the character Noodle from the virtual band Gorillaz.

To bring the animated group's guitarist to life, the singer went for an elaborate production full of visual references to the musical project's classic era. The highlight of the outfit was an exclusive latex piece developed by the brand VEX, known for its custom-molded costumes.

The complete look included a dress with red trim at the hem, combined with striped socks that directly reference the character's iconic outfit in the band's music videos. To top off the characterization, Billie completed the style with a blue bob-cut wig, faithfully reproducing Noodle's aesthetic.

The choice of costume was widely celebrated by the artist's fans and Gorillaz enthusiasts on social media. The intersection between the visual universe of the band created by Damon Albarn and Jamie Hewlett and Eilish's authentic style generated a major digital buzz.

Known for her creative and often surprising choices on Halloween, the singer usually steers clear of the conventional in her festive productions. In recent years, the artist has already shown an appreciation for pop culture references, animations, and cinema classics in her October celebrations.

So far, there is no official confirmation as to whether the artist attended any closed event in the costume or if the outfit was used solely for the photo shoot published on her official profiles. Either way, the tribute reinforces the star's strong connection to striking musical references from her artistic upbringing.

The post's reaction solidifies yet another moment of relaxed interaction between the singer and her audience, maintaining the tradition of surprising her global fan base with detailed visual productions during festive dates.
