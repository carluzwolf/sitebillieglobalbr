Billie Eilish enters YouTube's Billion Views Club with hit

The global smash hit BIRDS OF A FEATHER has reached the impressive milestone of one billion views on the video platform.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Nov 12, 2026 · 2 min read

The successful journey of the Hit Me Hard and Soft era continues to yield major milestones for Billie Eilish around the world. Recently, the artist achieved a new historical milestone in her digital career as one of her biggest recent anthems surpassed the one billion views mark on YouTube, further cementing her global impact on streaming platforms.

The track responsible for the feat is BIRDS OF A FEATHER, which has dominated music charts and fans' hearts since the release of the singer's third studio album. The project hit the market in May 2024 and quickly turned into an absolute phenomenon on social media as well as audio and video platforms, driven by its captivating sound and strong emotional appeal.

The achievement places the music video and official content associated with the song into the select group of productions that have broken the ten-digit view barrier on Google's platform. The skyrocketing reach reinforces the strong connection the singer maintains with her audience, who tirelessly consume her visual and auditory releases.

Besides Billie, the international music scene is seeing important movements on the charts and digital platforms during this period. Other major names in music are also celebrating expressive milestones in their respective careers, while seasonal year-end classics begin to return to global music consumption charts.

Ever since bursting onto the music scene with the instant success of Ocean Eyes in 2015, Billie Eilish has accumulated impressive streaming and sales records. The artist already collects several music videos in YouTube's prestigious billionaire list, including megahits like bad guy and lovely, demonstrating an enviable consistency over the years.

The Hit Me Hard and Soft Tour continues to draw crowds to sold-out arenas around the planet, allowing fans to experience these tracks live. The singer continues to balance her massive commercial success with an authentic artistic identity committed to environmental and social causes.
