Billie Eilish surprises fans by adopting a new strawberry blonde hair look

The singer opted for a radical change in appearance, abandoning her dark locks for a golden tone reminiscent of the Happier Than Ever era.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Oct 5, 2026 · 2 min read

Singer Billie Eilish left fans in a frenzy on social media by showing off a striking hair transformation. Known for transitioning between bold styles and iconic colors throughout her career, the artist opted for shorter locks in a soft golden shade, known as strawberry blonde.

The news immediately caught the attention of the global public and specialized press, marking a new cycle in the pop star's aesthetic. The revamped look directly recalls 2021, when she surprised the world by adopting platinum hair to promote the acclaimed album Happier Than Ever.

Since then, the interpreter of What Was I Made For? had returned to darker tones and deep browns, which became her trademark during the Hit Me Hard and Soft era. This constant alternation between visual palettes has always worked as a thermometer for the different artistic moments of the singer's career.

However, so far, there is no official confirmation whether this drastic change in her hair is associated with the development of a new music project, the start of a future tour, or if it is simply a personal style choice. The artist's team has not yet commented on the reasons behind the makeover.

Historically, Billie uses her image to mark important transitions in her music journey. The famous blonde phase of 2021, for example, symbolized a phase of greater maturity and artistic openness after the global phenomenon of her debut album, When We All Fall Asleep, Where Do We Go?.

Brazilian and international fans have already started speculating on social media about the meaning of the new color and what it might represent for the artist's next steps. It now remains to be seen whether the strawberry blonde is here to stay and what surprises Billie Eilish is preparing for the future.
