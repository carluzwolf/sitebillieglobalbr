Billie Eilish allocates R$ 58 million from tour to social and environmental causes

During an awards ceremony, the singer announced a million-dollar donation and made a strong plea regarding wealth distribution.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Oct 30, 2025 · 1 min read

Singer Billie Eilish surprised the audience and once again demonstrated her strong social engagement by announcing a million-dollar donation tied to her worldwide concert series. The artist allocated $11.5 million, equivalent to about R$ 58 million, to non-profit organizations focused on important global agendas.

The impactful announcement was made during the prestigious WSJ Magazine Innovator Awards. On the occasion, the interpreter of Bad Guy was honored with the Music Innovator of the Year trophy, celebrating both her artistic impact and her constant stance in favor of socio-environmental causes.

The funds raised from her tour will be distributed to initiatives that combat food insecurity, promote climate justice, and actively fight against carbon pollution. The choice of these fronts consolidates the pop star's historic commitment to sustainability and the preservation of the planet.

Upon taking the stage to receive the honor, the young artist used the platform to send a direct message to the moguls present in the audience. She questioned the concentration of wealth and encouraged billionaires to allocate part of their fortunes to help those who need it most.

The sincere speech generated mixed reactions among the illustrious guests packing the event in New York. While many celebrated her stance with applause, international press reports pointed out that figures like executive Mark Zuckerberg did not join in the clapping.

The musician's firm attitude was later reinforced by host Stephen Colbert, who led moments of the ceremony and publicly thanked the singer for her generosity. For Brazilian fans and admirers around the globe, the initiative reaffirms that the star's influence goes far beyond the stage and chart-topping hits.
