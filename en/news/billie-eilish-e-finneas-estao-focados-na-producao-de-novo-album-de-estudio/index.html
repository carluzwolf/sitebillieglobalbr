Billie Eilish and Finneas are focused on producing a new studio album

In a recent interview, the singer revealed that she and her brother are dedicated to developing a new musical project.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Oct 25, 2026 · 2 min read

The successful partnership between Billie Eilish and her brother and producer, Finneas, seems ready to bear new fruit soon. In an interview granted to the prestigious Vogue magazine, the artist opened up about the behind-the-scenes of her current routine and confirmed that the duo is totally immersed in the creation process of a new studio album.

Known for her meticulous dedication to the sound of her projects, Billie highlighted that the pace of work in the studio has intensified significantly. The singer explained that they are completely focused on the development phase and that the duo has been producing a large amount of unreleased material lately.

This movement in the studios comes after the resounding reception of her third studio album, Hit Me Hard and Soft, released in May 2024. The previous record won over the public and specialized critics with striking tracks like Birds of a Feather and Wildflower, further consolidating the unique sonic aesthetic built by the siblings over the years.

Despite the excitement generated by the revelation, fans will need to exercise patience. To date, Billie Eilish and her team have not released any official details about the title, tracklist, or possible release date of the highly anticipated successor to Hit Me Hard and Soft.

In addition to diving headfirst into the writing and production of new songs, Billie and Finneas have also been splitting their time with other creative initiatives. Recently, the duo drew attention by signing a sustainable jewelry collaboration in partnership with the brand John Hardy, showing that their talent goes beyond the stages and recording studios.

While the new album does not take definitive shape in the music market, rumors about possible major tours by the star for the coming years are growing behind the scenes of the industry. However, it is worth noting that there is no official confirmation from the artist or her record label regarding concert schedules or deadlines for the arrival of the new music.

The future project has the mission of continuing an impeccable discography that has accompanied Billie's trajectory since her adolescence. It now remains for fans to await the next steps of this familial partnership that continues to redefine the paths of global contemporary pop music.
