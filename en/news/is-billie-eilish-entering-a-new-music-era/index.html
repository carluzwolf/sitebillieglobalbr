Billie Eilish's new haircut excites fans and fuels rumors about the BE4 era

The artist's makeover has sparked speculation about the arrival of a new musical project as preparations for her fourth album move forward.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Oct 22, 2026 · 2 min read

Billie Eilish surprised her followers on social media by showing off a drastic makeover, adopting a light red bob cut. The news was shared in a video posted on Instagram, where the artist appears tossing her hair casually inside a car.

To accompany the post, the singer chose the song Cut Your Hair, released by the band Pavement in 1994, which immediately caught the public's attention. The chosen soundtrack, combined with the artist's history of marking career transitions with new visual identities, made fans start speculating whether a new artistic phase might be on the way.

Historically, every major aesthetic change in the star's trajectory has been associated with a new chapter in her discography. Although the caption featured only an emoji, the public quickly connected the hair transformation to the anticipated fourth studio album, a project she is developing alongside her brother and producer Finneas.

Studio work is well underway, with indications that the successor to Hit Me Hard and Soft could arrive in 2027. Finneas has previously commented that the creative process has sought inspiration from classic sounds, while industry reports point to plans for a large-scale tour between 2027 and 2028.

Before adopting the short cut with copper undertones, the artist had given hints of the new shade in a jewelry campaign co-created with her brother for the brand John Hardy. The collection features references to her recent discography and explores both delicate and bold visual elements.

The shade was also seen during rehearsals for Billie's foray into the acting world, in a production inspired by classic literature filmed in Toronto. With so many simultaneous developments in fashion, acting, and music, the fandom is keeping a close eye on every move the singer makes while awaiting the official announcement of the next cycle of her career.
