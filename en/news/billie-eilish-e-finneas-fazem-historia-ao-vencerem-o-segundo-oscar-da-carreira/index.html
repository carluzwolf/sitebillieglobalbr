Billie Eilish and Finneas Make History by Winning Their Second Career Oscar

At 22 years old, Billie Eilish and her brother Finneas secured their second Academy golden statuette in the best original song category.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Mar 10, 2024 · 2 min read

Sunday night went down in music and cinema history with a new impressive achievement for Billie Eilish and her brother and creative partner, Finneas O'Connell. At 22 and 26 years old, respectively, the duo reached an unprecedented feat at the Academy Awards by securing their second golden trophy before turning thirty years old.

The recognition came for the track What Was I Made For?, composed for the soundtrack of the blockbuster Barbie, directed by Greta Gerwig. The emotional ballad, which became a global streaming and critical phenomenon, had already accumulated other major awards of the season before shining on Hollywood's main stage.

Previously, the brothers had already won in the same category earlier in the decade. On that occasion, the victory came for No Time to Die, the official song for the James Bond film that also consolidated the singer's early talent in composing for major cinematic productions.

With this new achievement, Billie and Finneas enter an extremely select group of artists awarded twice by the Academy before reaching maturity at thirty. Legendary names in cinema, such as Jodie Foster, Hilary Swank, and Luise Rainer, also achieved double victories in their youth, but none of them at an age as young as the siblings.

During their acceptance speech on stage, the artist showed surprise at the announcement and made sure to thank director Greta Gerwig and star Margot Robbie. The singer stated she felt extremely lucky for the opportunity to be part of such a significant project for recent pop culture.

The competition in the category was fierce and featured major names from the music and film industry. Among the contenders that same night were heavy-hitting tracks like I'm Just Ken, also from the Barbie universe, alongside compositions performed by established artists in high-profile productions.

This new milestone reinforces the singer's artistic versatility and Finneas's genius in music production, proving that the family partnership is one of the most creative forces today. For the fans of Billie Global BR, this consecration at world cinema's biggest award ceremony corroborates the undeniable talent that continues to conquer global records and streams.
