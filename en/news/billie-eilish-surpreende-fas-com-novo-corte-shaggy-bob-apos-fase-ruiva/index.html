Billie Eilish surprises fans with new shaggy bob haircut after red hair phase

The American artist has changed her look once again and appeared with a modern layered cut, just weeks after dyeing her hair red.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Oct 22, 2026 · 2 min read

The chameleon-like Billie Eilish has once again caught the public's attention by showing off a new hair transformation. Known for moving between striking styles throughout her journey in pop music, the artist surprised fans by adopting a casual and edgy shaggy bob cut.

The news comes surprisingly fast after the singer's last major change. Just two weeks ago, she had revealed a radical transformation to her followers by adopting entirely reddish hair, marking a vibrant aesthetic that perfectly matched her most recent musical phase.

The new look with layered cuts and bangs evokes 1970s aesthetic references and alternative rock, styles that the songwriter often incorporates into her unique wardrobe. The speed with which she alternates between colors and cuts reinforces her artistic freedom and her taste for breaking visual expectations.

Since the beginning of her career, when she shocked the world by adopting iconic black hair with neon green roots during the era of her debut album When We All Fall Asleep, Where Do We Go?, Billie has turned every aesthetic change into a cultural event awaited by millions of admirers around the globe.

Her transition to the platinum blonde look in 2021, which inaugurated the Happier Than Ever era, and the return to darker tones during the promotion of the acclaimed album Hit Me Hard and Soft show that the star's image is in constant creative evolution.

For now, there is no official confirmation about the reasons behind the new hair change or whether the cut is part of the preparation for an unprecedented visual project, advertising campaign, or a new leg of her world tour.

Aware of the impact she causes on the internet, the artist shared the new style casually, immediately generating thousands of reactions on social media. Brazilian fans of the Billie Global BR portal remain attentive to the singer's next steps, as she continues to set trends both on and off the stage.
