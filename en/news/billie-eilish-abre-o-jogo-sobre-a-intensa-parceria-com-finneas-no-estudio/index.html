Billie Eilish opens up about her intense studio partnership with Finneas

During an event in London, the singer revealed candid details about her creative process alongside her brother and the challenges of songwriting.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Jul 10, 2025 · 2 min read

Billie Eilish's time in London has been marked by memorable moments with fans and deep revelations about her career. Last Wednesday, July ninth, the North American artist participated in an exclusive chat promoted by American Express at a brand store in the British capital. The event featured an interview conducted by presenter Edith Bowman and answered dozens of questions sent in by the audience.

The occasion served as a warmup for the marathon of sold-out shows the singer is performing at the O2 Arena. This series of performances is part of the world tour promoting her acclaimed third studio album, released last year, which has been captivating crowds across the globe.

During the conversation with fans, one of the central themes was her working relationship with her brother and longtime partner, Finneas. Known for producing and writing practically their entire repertoire together since the beginning of their musical journey, the two share a very unique dynamic inside the studio.

Billie was transparent in explaining that their family relationship allows for a unique frankness, but that this also brings intense friction. She pointed out that the freedom to say what she thinks is absolute, as there is the certainty that their sibling bonds and friendship will withstand any artistic disagreement.

According to the voice of *Happier Than Ever*, professional disagreements sometimes reach very harsh levels. However, she emphasized that this degree of intimacy and the unconditional love she feels for him make their creative connection irreplaceable, to the point where she sees no reason to collaborate with other people.

In addition to the dynamic with her brother, the pop star addressed the difficulties inherent in the act of songwriting. Surprising some of the fans present, she confessed that putting feelings into lyrics and melodies continues to require a monumental effort and generates moments of great personal frustration.

The artist explained that she frequently feels challenged when trying to translate exactly what she wants to express in her songs. Even after accumulating major hits and important awards over the years, the process of creating new tracks still brings insecurities that she constantly has to face.

Following the special event in London and the sequence of sold-out nights at the O2 Arena, the tour heads to other major cities, such as Manchester and Dublin. The intense schedule consolidates the ongoing success of the current era and maintains the strong connection between the singer and her international supporters.
