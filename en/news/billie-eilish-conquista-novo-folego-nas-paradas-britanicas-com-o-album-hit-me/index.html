Billie Eilish achieves a new breath on the British charts with the album Hit Me Hard and Soft

The singer's third studio album has climbed back up three important UK charts, demonstrating the ongoing strength of the record.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Sep 11, 2026 · 2 min read

The successful trajectory of the acclaimed album Hit Me Hard and Soft continues to yield impressive results for Billie Eilish around the world. Released in May 2024, the artist's third studio project has returned to prominent positions on three of the main UK record charts, proving that the repertoire continues to win over new audiences and further secure the loyalty of British fans.

The commercial impact and continuous consumption of the material on streaming platforms and in the physical market drove this expressive return. The singer's consistency on the official UK lists reflects the European public's enduring affection for her sonic aesthetic and sincere lyrics, which have marked a new phase in her artistic career.

Hit Me Hard and Soft hit the market preceded by high expectations following the global consecration of previous albums such as When We All Fall Asleep, Where Do We Go? and Happier Than Ever. The project further consolidated Billie's creative partnership with her brother and producer Finneas O'Connell, exploring experimental sounds that range through alternative pop, jazz, and electronic influences.

Since the album's release, the artist has taken her music to thousands of people around the globe with the world tour Hit Me Hard and Soft: The Tour. The series of live performances has been widely praised by specialized critics and the public due to the grandeur of the production and the strong environmental appeal adopted by the singer's team.

Despite the strong performance on recent charts, Billie Eilish's team has not yet released new official details about future commercial developments or the release of new singles focused exclusively on the current promotion. It is important to emphasize that any additional plans for this album cycle still await official confirmation from the record label or the artist herself.

For fans who follow every step of the star's journey, the moment is one of celebration for the ongoing records. The dedication of the global community ensures that the legacy of Hit Me Hard and Soft remains alive and relevant on international charts for much longer.
