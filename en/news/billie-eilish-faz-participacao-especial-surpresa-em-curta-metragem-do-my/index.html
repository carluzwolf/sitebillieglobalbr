Billie Eilish makes surprise cameo in My Chemical Romance short film

The singer participates with voice acting in the animated short film Prelude To The End, released in celebration of the 20th anniversary of the album The Black Parade.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Oct 30, 2026 · 2 min read

Billie Eilish's influence on pop culture continues to break boundaries and now reaches the alternative rock universe in a very special way. The American artist made her presence known in an unprecedented project led by My Chemical Romance, one of the most iconic bands of the 2000s.

Gerard Way's group prepared a surprise for fans by releasing the animated short film titled Prelude To The End. The audiovisual production was created to celebrate the twentieth anniversary of the acclaimed record The Black Parade, which received a commemorative deluxe edition containing bonus tracks and rare 2007 recordings.

The direction of the animation was handled by Lauren Tsai, who also authored the project through her studio, Unreal Inc. The story acts as an expansion of the short film The Dying World and serves as a visual introduction to the band's classic album repertoire.

In the plot, the protagonist Astrid, voiced by the director herself, experiences a sequence of surrealist events at school that culminates in a journey inside her own drawings. It is precisely at the beginning of this odyssey, during a mysterious phone call, that the audience can hear contributions from major names in today's music.

Billie Eilish appears in the production voicing a friend concerned about the protagonist. In addition to her, singer Phoebe Bridgers also lends her voice to play Astrid's sister in a brief scene that nevertheless generated huge positive repercussions among fans of both artists on social media.

The short film's voice cast also features unusual appearances, such as actor John C. McGinley and famous game designer Hideo Kojima. The director publicly highlighted the honor of collaborating with My Chemical Romance's creative legacy in such a milestone work for rock history.

The screening of the animated project takes place during the final stretch of the band's concert residency at the historic Hollywood Bowl. So far, there is no official information regarding the wide release of the animation on open digital platforms for the general public.
