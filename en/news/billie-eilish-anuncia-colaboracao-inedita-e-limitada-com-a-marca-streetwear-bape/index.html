Billie Eilish announces unprecedented and limited collaboration with streetwear brand BAPE

The singer's iconic oversized aesthetic meets classic Japanese streetwear in an exclusive clothing and accessories line.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Nov 12, 2026 · 1 min read

The long-standing relationship between Billie Eilish and the Japanese brand A Bathing Ape, better known as BAPE, has finally resulted in an official fashion partnership. Fans of the artist and streetwear enthusiasts can now prepare for the launch of a limited collaborative collection that brings together the singer's unmistakable style and the brand's urban heritage.

World-renowned for setting trends with her baggy clothes and striking visuals, Billie has always shown an appreciation for pieces with a skater and urban vibe. This long-standing aesthetic affinity now comes to life commercially through a carefully curated selection of apparel and collectible accessories.

The news was announced to the public as a surprise, generating immense anticipation on social media. The line hits the global market on November 13th, bringing exclusive designs developed jointly with the Japanese brand's creative team.

To give even more prominence to the partnership, it was confirmed that two of the collection's main designs will be available exclusively through the Complex platform. This strategy reinforces the singer's connection with the universe of pop culture and urban collecting.

Not all details regarding the prices of the pieces or whether there will be physical distribution in selected stores in Brazil have been released yet. Therefore, Brazilian fans who want to secure the items should keep an eye on the official international sales channels.

This union further solidifies Billie Eilish's influence in the fashion world, proving that her impact goes far beyond the stage and the music charts. More information about stock and future restocks should be announced soon by the brands involved.
