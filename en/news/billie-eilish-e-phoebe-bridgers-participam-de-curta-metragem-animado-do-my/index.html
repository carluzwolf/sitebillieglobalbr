Billie Eilish and Phoebe Bridgers participate in My Chemical Romance animated short film

The artists are part of the visual project Prelude to the End, released by the band with illustrations by artist Lauren Tsai.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Nov 1, 2026 · 2 min read

The My Chemical Romance universe gained a new and surprising visual chapter with the release of the project titled Prelude to the End. The production immediately caught the attention of fans from different generations by featuring heavyweights in today's music, including singer-songwriter Billie Eilish and indie artist Phoebe Bridgers.

The animated short film is the result of a creative collaboration with illustrator and artist Lauren Tsai, known for her striking style. The work features a dark and captivating visual aesthetic, elements that directly dialogue with the artistic identity built by My Chemical Romance throughout their trajectory in alternative rock and emo.

Although specific details about the exact roles of Billie Eilish and Phoebe Bridgers in the animated narrative have not yet been fully detailed in official statements, the presence of both artists generated enormous repercussion on social media. Fans of both singers and the band celebrated the unexpected union between their musical universes.

Billie Eilish has increasingly demonstrated versatility in her side projects and visual partnerships, consolidating her space not only in alternative pop, but also in collaborations that cross different branches of pop culture and rock. The artist continues to reap the rewards of her solid career and critically acclaimed releases.

In turn, Phoebe Bridgers brings her melancholic and intimate signature to the project, perfectly complementing the dramatic atmosphere proposed by My Chemical Romance in this new visual phase. The collaboration with Lauren Tsai reinforces the aesthetic care that the band has always imprinted on their music videos and conceptual productions.

To date, the official representatives of the involved artists have not disclosed whether the short film will be part of a larger campaign or a new full-length album by the band. More information about developments in this partnership is expected to be announced soon through My Chemical Romance's official channels.
