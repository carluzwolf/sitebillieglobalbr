Billie Eilish surprises fans by showing rare tattoos in new social media photo

The singer stirred up the internet by sharing unpublished records revealing details of her stomach and hand tattoos.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Oct 27, 2026 · 2 min read

Known for keeping much of her personal life and body discreetly covered over the years, Billie Eilish recently caught the attention of the international and Brazilian fandom. The pop star caused a stir on social media by publishing a new image that offers a rare glimpse of her tattoo collection, surprising the most attentive admirers.

Although the artist has mentioned in past interviews that she has a few designs on her body, she tends to be quite reserved about showing them publicly. This time, however, the shared photograph subtly yet clearly highlighted the bold body art she carries in the stomach region, in addition to detailed markings on her hands.

Billie's relationship with tattoos has always sparked curiosity among fans. The first time she officially confirmed having a tattoo was in 2020, shortly after the release of her debut album, when she revealed she had inked her own last name in gothic letters on her chest. Since then, the singer has gradually and very privately added new lines to her skin.

Last year, the "Happier Than Ever" singer also made headlines by showing a huge dragon covering a large part of her back, along with other abstract compositions. Each new appearance of these designs is usually celebrated by fans, who try to decipher the meanings and style of each stroke chosen by the Grammy winner.

The new post quickly circulated on platforms like X (formerly Twitter) and Instagram, where fan clubs around the world, including Billie Global BR, enthusiastically shared the image. For the fanbase, any unpublished detail about the singer's daily life or look turns into a major internet event.

As she prepares for the next steps of her career and tours, the artist continues to prove that she knows exactly how to capture the public's attention, whether through her critically acclaimed music or small glimpses of her visual identity off the stage and red carpets.
