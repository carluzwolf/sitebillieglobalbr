Billie Eilish surprises fans with bold look and relaxed photos on social media

A recent post brought back photos where the singer wears low-cut tops from the brand MIAOU, generating a huge reaction among admirers.

By Billie Global BR Newsroom · Oct 31, 2026 · 1 min read

Billie Eilish's aesthetic has always been one of the biggest talking points among her followers around the world. Initially known for wearing baggy, oversized clothes to avoid judgment about her body, the artist has shown increasing versatility in her fashion choices in recent years.

Recently, a post originally shared late last year began circulating heavily on platforms like Reddit. In the image, the voice behind Ocean Eyes appears in a kitchen wearing a polka dot thin-strap top by the brand MIAOU, completing the look with a gold necklace and shoulder-length hair.

What caught fans' attention the most was the fact that the singer posed in a relaxed way while holding a small baby bottle. Shortly after, in January of this year, similar records with the same piece of clothing and a cookie continued to delight the public, accumulating millions of views and tens of thousands of likes.

These relaxed moments recall other occasions when the artist caused a buzz on the internet by opting for tighter designs. In July 2023, for example, a photo of her wearing a colorful bikini from the same fashion house and Versace sunglasses had already won hundreds of thousands of positive reactions on Instagram.

The repercussion surrounding these publications proves that any move by the Grammy winner is closely followed by her loyal fan base. Whether with striking outfits at major award shows or in home snapshots, Billie continues to set trends and break the internet with her authenticity.
